GALSEN IA VOLET 1 - MASTER CONSTITUTION CHAPTER 09 - PROJECT PHILOSOPHY
Version: 1.1 Language: English

PURPOSE This chapter defines the permanent philosophy behind every
decision made during the development of GalSen IA.

CORE PHILOSOPHY GalSen IA is built to solve real-world problems, not to
demonstrate technology.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES - Simplicity before complexity. - Reliability before
novelty. - Long-term value before short-term gains. - Maintainability
before rapid expansion. - User benefit before technical elegance.

USER-CENTERED DEVELOPMENT Every feature must answer: - Who benefits? -
What problem is solved? - Is the solution sustainable? - Can it be
maintained? - Can it scale?

ENGINEERING PRINCIPLES - Build only what is useful. - Remove unnecessary
complexity. - Reuse before rewriting. - Automate repetitive tasks. -
Document important decisions.

COLLABORATION Encourage clear communication, traceable decisions and
continuous learning.

FINAL RULE Technology is a tool. The success of GalSen IA is measured by
the value it creates for people, organizations and society.

END OF CHAPTER 09
