GALSEN IA VOLET 1 - MASTER CONSTITUTION CHAPTER 05 - LONG-TERM VISION
Version: 1.1 Language: English

PURPOSE This chapter defines the long-term direction of GalSen IA over
the next decades.

VISION GalSen IA is not a short-term application. It is a long-term
intelligent platform designed to evolve continuously.

FOUNDATION Every architectural decision must support: - Scalability -
Reliability - Security - Maintainability - Extensibility - Simplicity

EVOLUTION The platform should evolve by adding new capabilities without
rebuilding the core engine.

MODULARITY Every new feature should be implemented as an independent
module whenever practical.

COUNTRY EXPANSION The core platform remains the same. Only
country-specific knowledge, regulations and integrations change.

FUTURE TECHNOLOGIES The architecture should be prepared for: - Advanced
AI models - Robotics - Drones - Computer Vision - Voice systems - Smart
devices - Government services - Enterprise integrations

SUSTAINABILITY Technical debt should be minimized. Temporary solutions
should eventually be replaced with robust implementations.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Every generation of developers must leave GalSen IA in a
better state than they found it.

END OF CHAPTER 05
