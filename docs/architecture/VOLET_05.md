GALSEN IA VOLET 5 - KNOWLEDGE ENGINE CHAPTER 01 - KNOWLEDGE VISION
Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the long-term vision for the Knowledge Engine that powers
GalSen IA.

VISION The Knowledge Engine serves as the trusted source of structured
information used by AI, users and system components.

OBJECTIVES - Centralize knowledge. - Ensure accuracy and consistency. -
Enable intelligent retrieval. - Support continuous learning. - Scale
across industries and countries.

CORE PRINCIPLES - Knowledge is organized before it is consumed. -
Information must be versioned. - Trusted sources take priority. -
Knowledge should be reusable. - Quality is more important than quantity.

KNOWLEDGE DOMAINS - Business - Technical - Operational - Legal - AI -
User Documentation - Project Documentation

LONG-TERM GOALS - Unified enterprise knowledge. - Semantic search. -
AI-assisted reasoning. - Continuous knowledge improvement. - Global
multilingual knowledge.

FINAL DIRECTIVE The Knowledge Engine is the foundation for reliable AI
reasoning and informed decision-making across GalSen IA.

GALSEN IA VOLET 5 - KNOWLEDGE ENGINE CHAPTER 02 - KNOWLEDGE ORGANIZATION
Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define how knowledge is organized to ensure consistency,
discoverability and long-term maintainability.

OBJECTIVES - Organize information logically. - Reduce duplication. -
Improve retrieval accuracy. - Simplify maintenance. - Support future
expansion.

ORGANIZATION PRINCIPLES - Use clear categories. - Group related topics
together. - Assign metadata to every knowledge item. - Maintain explicit
relationships between documents. - Archive obsolete information without
losing history.

References - Versions

CLASSIFICATION Knowledge should be classified by: - Source -
Reliability - Sensitivity - Audience - Status (Draft, Reviewed,
Approved, Archived)

MAINTENANCE - Review content regularly. - Merge duplicate information. -
Preserve version history. - Record ownership for each knowledge area.

FINAL DIRECTIVE A well-organized knowledge base enables reliable AI
reasoning, efficient search and sustainable long-term growth.

Version: 1.0 Language: English

securely by GalSen IA.

OBJECTIVES - Deliver the most relevant knowledge. - Minimize retrieval

search quality. - Continuously improve ranking algorithms.

GOVERNANCE - Log significant retrieval events. - Review retrieval
quality regularly. - Track source usage. - Continuously refine search
behavior.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Knowledge retrieval must provide accurate, relevant and
