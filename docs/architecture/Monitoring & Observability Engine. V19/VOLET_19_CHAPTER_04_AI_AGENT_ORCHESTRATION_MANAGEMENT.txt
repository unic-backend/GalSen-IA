GALSEN IA VOLET 19 - AI AGENT ORCHESTRATION ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 04 -
AI AGENT ORCHESTRATION MANAGEMENT Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the management framework for AI agent orchestration,
coordination and execution across the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES - Centralize orchestration administration. - Manage AI agent
resources. - Control orchestration policies. - Ensure operational
reliability. - Support lifecycle governance.

MANAGEMENT DOMAINS - Agent Management. - Task Management. - Resource
Management. - Orchestration Management. - Policy Management. -
Orchestration Audit.

MANAGEMENT PRINCIPLES - Reliability. - Scalability. - Security. -
Accountability. - Continuous Monitoring.

OPERATIONAL PROCESS 1. Register AI agents. 2. Configure orchestration
policies. 3. Assign tasks. 4. Monitor execution. 5. Optimize
orchestration performance. 6. Archive completed orchestration records.

QUALITY CONTROLS - Agent validation. - Execution monitoring. - Policy
verification. - Audit logging. - Performance assessment.

FINAL DIRECTIVE AI Agent Orchestration Management shall ensure secure,
centralized and auditable control of all orchestration services
throughout the GalSen IA ecosystem.
