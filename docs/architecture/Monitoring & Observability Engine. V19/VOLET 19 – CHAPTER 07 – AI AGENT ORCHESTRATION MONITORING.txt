VOLET 19 - AI AGENT ORCHESTRATION ENGINE MANUAL
CHAPTER 07 - AI AGENT ORCHESTRATION COMPLIANCE

Version: 1.0
Language: English

PURPOSE

Define the compliance framework for AI agent orchestration, coordination and autonomous execution across the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES

- Ensure regulatory compliance.
- Protect orchestration data.
- Maintain audit readiness.
- Enforce governance policies.
- Support continuous compliance improvement.

COMPLIANCE PRINCIPLES

- Accountability.
- Transparency.
- Privacy by Design.
- Auditability.
- Continuous Improvement.
- Responsible AI.

COMPLIANCE DOMAINS

- AI Governance.
- Data Protection.
- Agent Accountability.
- Execution Logging.
- Regulatory Reporting.
- Policy Enforcement.
- Risk Management.
- Ethical AI Compliance.

COMPLIANCE PROCESS

1. Define compliance requirements.
2. Implement orchestration policies.
3. Monitor compliance status.
4. Perform regular audits.
5. Correct non-compliance.
6. Improve compliance continuously.
7. Validate AI governance.
8. Document compliance evidence.

QUALITY CONTROLS

- Policy reviews.
- Compliance reporting.
- Audit validation.
- Risk assessments.
- Control verification.
- AI ethics reviews.
- Governance validation.

FINAL DIRECTIVE

AI Agent Orchestration Compliance shall ensure that all orchestration services operate in accordance with governance standards, security policies, ethical AI principles and regulatory obligations throughout the GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 07
