VOLET 19 - AI AGENT ORCHESTRATION ENGINE MANUAL
CHAPTER 08 - AI AGENT ORCHESTRATION GOVERNANCE

Version: 1.0
Language: English

PURPOSE

Define the governance framework for AI agent orchestration, coordination and autonomous execution across the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES

- Establish governance responsibilities.
- Ensure strategic alignment.
- Maintain security and compliance.
- Support accountability.
- Drive continuous improvement.
- Govern autonomous AI behavior.

GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLES

- Accountability.
- Transparency.
- Policy-driven management.
- Risk awareness.
- Human oversight.
- Continuous review.
- Responsible AI.

ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

- AI Governance Board.
- AI Orchestration Administrators.
- Security Team.
- Compliance Officers.
- System Owners.
- AI Ethics Committee.
- Audit Team.

GOVERNANCE PROCESS

1. Define orchestration policies.
2. Review governance objectives.
3. Approve orchestration changes.
4. Monitor governance metrics.
5. Audit compliance.
6. Assess AI risks.
7. Improve governance continuously.
8. Review strategic alignment.

QUALITY CONTROLS

- Governance reviews.
- Policy validation.
- Audit reporting.
- Risk assessments.
- Continuous monitoring.
- Ethics reviews.
- Performance governance.

FINAL DIRECTIVE

AI Agent Orchestration Governance shall ensure secure, accountable, transparent and policy-driven orchestration services while maintaining responsible AI practices throughout the entire GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 08
