GALSEN IA VOLET 17 - NOTIFICATION ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 04 -
NOTIFICATION ENGINE MANAGEMENT Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the management framework for notification services,
channels and delivery operations across the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES - Centralize notification administration. - Manage delivery
channels. - Control notification templates. - Ensure operational
reliability. - Support lifecycle governance.

MANAGEMENT DOMAINS - Channel Management. - Template Management. -
Subscription Management. - Delivery Management. - Queue Management. -
Notification Audit.

MANAGEMENT PRINCIPLES - Reliability. - Scalability. - Security. -
Accountability. - Continuous Monitoring.

OPERATIONAL PROCESS 1. Register notification channels. 2. Configure
templates. 3. Manage subscriptions. 4. Process delivery queues. 5.
Monitor notification status. 6. Archive completed notifications.

QUALITY CONTROLS - Delivery validation. - Queue health monitoring. -
Template reviews. - Audit logging. - Performance verification.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Notification Engine Management shall ensure secure,
centralized and auditable control of all notification services
throughout the GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 04
