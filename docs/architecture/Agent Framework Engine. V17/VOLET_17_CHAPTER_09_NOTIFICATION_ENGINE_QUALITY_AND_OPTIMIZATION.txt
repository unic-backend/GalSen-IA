Capacity planning. - Root cause analysis. - Periodic quality reviews.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Notification Engine Quality and Optimization shall
continuously improve the reliability, efficiency, scalability and
operational excellence of notification services throughout the GalSen IA
ecosystem.
