GALSEN IA VOLET 17 - NOTIFICATION ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 08 -
NOTIFICATION ENGINE GOVERNANCE Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the governance framework for notification services,
communication channels and messaging operations across the GalSen IA
ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES - Establish governance responsibilities. - Ensure strategic
alignment. - Maintain security and compliance. - Support
accountability. - Drive continuous improvement.

GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLES - Accountability. - Transparency. - Policy-driven
management. - Risk awareness. - Continuous review.

ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES - Notification Governance Board. - Operations
Team. - Security Team. - Compliance Officers. - System Owners. - Audit
Team.

GOVERNANCE PROCESS 1. Define notification policies. 2. Review governance
objectives. 3. Approve major changes. 4. Monitor governance metrics. 5.
Audit compliance. 6. Continuously improve governance.

QUALITY CONTROLS - Governance reviews. - Policy validation. - Audit
reporting. - Risk assessments. - Continuous monitoring.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Notification Engine Governance shall ensure secure,
accountable and policy-driven notification services across the entire
GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 08
