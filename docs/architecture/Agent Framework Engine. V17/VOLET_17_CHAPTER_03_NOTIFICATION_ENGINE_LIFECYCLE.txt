GALSEN IA VOLET 17 - NOTIFICATION ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 03 -
NOTIFICATION ENGINE LIFECYCLE Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the complete lifecycle of notification services from
event generation to delivery, monitoring and archival.

OBJECTIVES - Standardize notification operations. - Ensure reliable
message delivery. - Maintain traceability. - Optimize delivery
performance. - Enable continuous improvement.

LIFECYCLE STAGES 1. Event Detection. 2. Notification Creation. 3. Rule
Validation. 4. Channel Selection. 5. Message Delivery. 6. Delivery
Confirmation. 7. Monitoring and Analytics. 8. Retention and Archival.

LIFECYCLE PRINCIPLES - Reliability. - Timeliness. - Security. -
Scalability. - Traceability.

MANAGEMENT PRACTICES - Validate events before processing. - Prioritize
critical notifications. - Retry failed deliveries. - Record delivery
history. - Archive completed notifications securely.

QUALITY CONTROLS - Delivery validation. - Queue monitoring. - Failure
analysis. - Audit logging. - Performance monitoring.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Every notification shall follow a governed lifecycle
that guarantees secure, reliable and traceable communication throughout
the GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 03
