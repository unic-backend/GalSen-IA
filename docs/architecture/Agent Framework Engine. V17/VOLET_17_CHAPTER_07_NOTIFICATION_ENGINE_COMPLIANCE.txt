GALSEN IA VOLET 17 - NOTIFICATION ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 07 -
NOTIFICATION ENGINE COMPLIANCE Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the compliance framework for notification services,
communication channels and messaging operations across the GalSen IA
ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES - Ensure regulatory compliance. - Protect notification
data. - Maintain audit readiness. - Enforce governance policies. -
Support continuous compliance improvement.

COMPLIANCE PRINCIPLES - Accountability. - Transparency. - Privacy by
Design. - Auditability. - Continuous Improvement.

COMPLIANCE DOMAINS - Notification governance. - Data protection. -
Message retention. - Audit logging. - Regulatory reporting. - Access
control.

COMPLIANCE PROCESS 1. Define compliance requirements. 2. Implement
notification policies. 3. Monitor compliance status. 4. Perform regular
audits. 5. Correct non-compliance. 6. Improve compliance continuously.

QUALITY CONTROLS - Policy reviews. - Compliance reporting. - Audit
validation. - Risk assessments. - Control verification.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Notification Engine Compliance shall ensure that all
notification services operate in accordance with security policies,
governance standards and regulatory obligations throughout the GalSen IA
ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 07
