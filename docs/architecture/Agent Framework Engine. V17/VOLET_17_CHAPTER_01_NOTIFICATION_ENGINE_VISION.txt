GALSEN IA VOLET 17 - NOTIFICATION ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 01 -
NOTIFICATION ENGINE VISION Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the vision, mission and strategic objectives of the
Notification Engine within the GalSen IA ecosystem.

VISION Provide intelligent, reliable and real-time notification services
for users, agents, systems and external integrations.

MISSION Deliver secure, scalable and event-driven notifications across
every communication channel.

OBJECTIVES - Centralize notification management. - Support multi-channel
delivery. - Enable real-time communication. - Improve user engagement. -
Ensure reliability and scalability.

CORE PRINCIPLES - Reliability. - Timeliness. - Security. -
Personalization. - Scalability. - Continuous improvement.

STRATEGIC CAPABILITIES - Email notifications. - SMS notifications. -
Push notifications. - In-app notifications. - Webhook events. -
Notification analytics.

FINAL DIRECTIVE The Notification Engine shall provide secure, scalable
and intelligent notification services throughout the GalSen IA
ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 01
