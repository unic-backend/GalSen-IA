GALSEN IA VOLET 17 - NOTIFICATION ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 10 -
NOTIFICATION ENGINE GOVERNANCE Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the enterprise governance model for the Notification
Engine to ensure long-term sustainability, reliability and strategic
alignment.

OBJECTIVES - Ensure enterprise-wide notification governance. - Maintain
operational excellence. - Support regulatory compliance. - Enable
continuous evolution. - Align notification services with business
strategy.

GOVERNANCE FRAMEWORK - Strategic oversight. - Operational governance. -
Risk management. - Compliance management. - Service lifecycle
governance. - Continuous improvement.

GOVERNANCE RESPONSIBILITIES - Define notification strategy. - Approve
major changes. - Monitor service KPIs. - Review security and
compliance. - Coordinate cross-system integration. - Drive continuous
optimization.

SUCCESS METRICS - Notification availability. - Delivery reliability. -
Security compliance. - Operational efficiency. - User satisfaction. -
Continuous improvement outcomes.

FINAL DIRECTIVE The Notification Engine shall operate under a unified
governance framework that guarantees secure, reliable, scalable and
continuously improving notification services throughout the GalSen IA
ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 10
