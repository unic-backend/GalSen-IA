GalSen IA shall function as a unified enterprise artificial intelligence platform where every engine, service, AI agent, workflow, decision, memory, knowledge base and business capability collaborates through a secure, intelligent, scalable and continuously evolving architecture designed to serve organizations at local, continental and global scale.

This directive supersedes all subsystem directives and serves as the highest architectural authority governing the complete GalSen IA ecosystem.
