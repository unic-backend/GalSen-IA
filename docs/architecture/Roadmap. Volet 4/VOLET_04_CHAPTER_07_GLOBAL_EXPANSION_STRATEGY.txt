GALSEN IA VOLET 4 - ROADMAP CHAPTER 07 - GLOBAL EXPANSION STRATEGY
Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the strategy for expanding GalSen IA into multiple
countries, markets and industries while preserving a unified platform.

OBJECTIVES - Support international growth. - Adapt to local
regulations. - Enable multilingual experiences. - Maintain a shared core
architecture. - Scale operations sustainably.

EXPANSION PRINCIPLES - Keep one global platform. - Localize without
fragmenting the core. - Respect regional legal requirements. - Reuse
common modules whenever possible. - Validate each market before
expansion.

LOCALIZATION - Multiple languages. - Local currencies. - Regional date
and time formats. - Country-specific workflows. - Local compliance
requirements.

MARKET ENTRY PROCESS 1. Market research 2. Regulatory assessment 3.
Localization 4. Pilot deployment 5. User feedback 6. Full rollout

SUCCESS METRICS - User adoption - Customer satisfaction - Platform
stability - Operational efficiency - Sustainable regional growth

FINAL DIRECTIVE Global expansion must strengthen the GalSen IA ecosystem
while preserving architectural consistency, security and long-term
maintainability.

END OF CHAPTER 07
