GALSEN IA VOLET 4 - ROADMAP CHAPTER 09 - SUCCESS METRICS AND KPIs
Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the key metrics used to measure the success, health and
long-term progress of GalSen IA.

OBJECTIVES - Measure meaningful outcomes. - Support data-driven
decisions. - Track strategic progress. - Identify improvement
opportunities. - Maintain transparency.

KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS - User adoption - User retention - Platform
availability - AI response quality - System performance - Security
incidents - Feature delivery rate - Customer satisfaction

ENGINEERING METRICS - Deployment frequency - Change failure rate - Mean
time to recovery (MTTR) - Test coverage - Technical debt trend - Defect
rate

BUSINESS METRICS - Active organizations - Regional expansion - Partner
integrations - Operational efficiency - Long-term sustainability

REVIEW PROCESS - Monitor KPIs regularly. - Investigate significant
deviations. - Adjust priorities using objective evidence. - Document
major findings and corrective actions.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Metrics exist to improve decisions and product quality,
not to optimize numbers at the expense of long-term value.

END OF CHAPTER 09
