GALSEN IA VOLET 4 - ROADMAP CHAPTER 08 - LONG-TERM SUSTAINABILITY
Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the principles that ensure GalSen IA remains sustainable,
maintainable and valuable over decades.

OBJECTIVES - Preserve long-term viability. - Balance innovation with
stability. - Manage resources responsibly. - Continuously improve the
platform. - Protect the project’s core vision.

SUSTAINABILITY PRINCIPLES - Build for longevity. - Minimize technical
debt. - Encourage modular evolution. - Invest in documentation. -
Prioritize maintainability over short-term speed.

RESOURCE MANAGEMENT - Optimize infrastructure usage. - Monitor
operational costs. - Reuse existing components. - Automate repetitive
processes. - Plan capacity proactively.

CONTINUOUS EVOLUTION - Review architecture regularly. - Retire obsolete
technologies responsibly. - Adopt proven innovations. - Improve
operational efficiency.

RISK MANAGEMENT - Identify long-term risks. - Maintain recovery
strategies. - Preserve critical knowledge. - Regularly review business
continuity plans.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Every decision should strengthen the long-term
sustainability of GalSen IA while preserving quality, security and
adaptability.

END OF CHAPTER 08
