GALSEN IA VOLET 4 - ROADMAP CHAPTER 01 - PRODUCT VISION Version: 1.0
Language: English

PURPOSE Define the long-term product vision that guides the evolution of
GalSen IA.

VISION GalSen IA will become a modular AI platform capable of supporting
businesses, organizations and individuals through intelligent, secure
and scalable services.

OBJECTIVES - Build a unified AI ecosystem. - Deliver practical value. -
Expand progressively across industries. - Support international
growth. - Remain adaptable to future technologies.

STRATEGIC PRIORITIES - Strong architectural foundation. - High-quality
user experience. - Reliable AI orchestration. - Secure data
management. - Continuous innovation.

ROADMAP PRINCIPLES - Deliver in incremental phases. - Validate before
expanding. - Prioritize user value. - Measure progress objectively. -
Preserve long-term sustainability.

SUCCESS INDICATORS - Stable platform. - Growing adoption. - High
reliability. - Continuous improvement. - Sustainable evolution.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Every roadmap decision must align with the long-term
vision rather than short-term opportunities.

END OF CHAPTER 01
