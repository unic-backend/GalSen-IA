GALSEN IA VOLET 4 - ROADMAP CHAPTER 10 - ROADMAP GOVERNANCE Version: 1.0
Language: English

PURPOSE Define how the GalSen IA roadmap is governed, reviewed and
updated over time.

OBJECTIVES - Maintain strategic alignment. - Ensure transparent
decision-making. - Control roadmap changes. - Preserve long-term
vision. - Enable continuous improvement.

GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLES - Review the roadmap regularly. - Base decisions
on evidence. - Record significant changes. - Communicate approved
updates. - Protect architectural consistency.

ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES - Product Leadership defines strategic
direction. - Architecture Leadership validates technical alignment. -
Engineering plans implementation. - Stakeholders provide feedback. -
Governance approves major roadmap changes.

REVIEW CYCLE - Evaluate milestones. - Measure KPIs. - Assess risks. -
Reprioritize initiatives when justified. - Publish updated roadmap
versions.

CHANGE CONTROL - Document every major roadmap revision. - Preserve
historical versions. - Explain the rationale for significant changes. -
Avoid unnecessary roadmap instability.

FINAL DIRECTIVE The roadmap is a living document that must evolve
responsibly while preserving the long-term mission of GalSen IA.
