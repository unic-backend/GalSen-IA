GALSEN IA VOLET 18 - WORKFLOW ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 03 - WORKFLOW ENGINE
LIFECYCLE Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the complete lifecycle of workflow execution from
creation to retirement across the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES - Standardize workflow operations. - Ensure reliable
execution. - Maintain traceability. - Optimize workflow performance. -
Enable continuous improvement.

LIFECYCLE STAGES 1. Workflow Design. 2. Workflow Validation. 3.
Deployment. 4. Execution. 5. Monitoring. 6. Optimization. 7. Version
Management. 8. Retirement and Archival.

LIFECYCLE PRINCIPLES - Reliability. - Automation. - Scalability. -
Traceability. - Security.

MANAGEMENT PRACTICES - Validate workflows before deployment. - Monitor
execution continuously. - Retry failed tasks safely. - Record execution
history. - Archive retired workflows securely.

QUALITY CONTROLS - Workflow validation. - Execution monitoring. -
Failure analysis. - Audit logging. - Performance monitoring.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Every workflow shall follow a governed lifecycle that
guarantees secure, reliable and traceable execution throughout the
GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 03
