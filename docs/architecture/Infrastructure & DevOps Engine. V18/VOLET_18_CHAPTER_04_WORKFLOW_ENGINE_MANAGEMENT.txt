GALSEN IA VOLET 18 - WORKFLOW ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 04 - WORKFLOW ENGINE
MANAGEMENT Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the management framework for workflow services,
orchestration and execution across the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES - Centralize workflow administration. - Manage workflow
definitions. - Control execution processes. - Ensure operational
reliability. - Support lifecycle governance.

MANAGEMENT DOMAINS - Workflow Management. - Task Management. - Rule
Management. - Execution Management. - Queue Management. - Workflow
Audit.

MANAGEMENT PRINCIPLES - Reliability. - Scalability. - Automation. -
Accountability. - Continuous Monitoring.

OPERATIONAL PROCESS 1. Register workflows. 2. Configure execution rules.
3. Schedule workflow tasks. 4. Monitor workflow execution. 5. Optimize
performance. 6. Archive completed workflows.

QUALITY CONTROLS - Workflow validation. - Execution monitoring. - Queue
health checks. - Audit logging. - Performance verification.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Workflow Engine Management shall ensure secure,
centralized and auditable control of all workflow services throughout
the GalSen IA ecosystem.
