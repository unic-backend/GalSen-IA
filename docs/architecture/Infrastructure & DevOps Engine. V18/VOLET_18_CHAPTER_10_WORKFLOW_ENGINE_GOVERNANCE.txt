GALSEN IA VOLET 18 - WORKFLOW ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 10 - WORKFLOW ENGINE
GOVERNANCE Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the enterprise governance model for the Workflow Engine
to ensure long-term sustainability, operational excellence and strategic
alignment.

OBJECTIVES - Ensure enterprise-wide workflow governance. - Maintain
operational excellence. - Support regulatory compliance. - Enable
continuous evolution. - Align workflow services with business strategy.

GOVERNANCE FRAMEWORK - Strategic oversight. - Operational governance. -
Risk management. - Compliance management. - Workflow lifecycle
governance. - Continuous improvement.

GOVERNANCE RESPONSIBILITIES - Define workflow strategy. - Approve major
workflow changes. - Monitor workflow KPIs. - Review security and
compliance. - Coordinate cross-system orchestration. - Drive continuous
optimization.

SUCCESS METRICS - Workflow availability. - Execution reliability. -
Security compliance. - Operational efficiency. - Automation
effectiveness. - Continuous improvement outcomes.

FINAL DIRECTIVE The Workflow Engine shall operate under a unified
governance framework that guarantees secure, reliable, scalable and
continuously improving workflow services throughout the GalSen IA
ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 10
