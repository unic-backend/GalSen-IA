GALSEN IA VOLET 18 - WORKFLOW ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 07 - WORKFLOW ENGINE
COMPLIANCE Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the compliance framework for workflow orchestration,
execution and automation services across the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES - Ensure regulatory compliance. - Protect workflow data. -
Maintain audit readiness. - Enforce governance policies. - Support
continuous compliance improvement.

COMPLIANCE PRINCIPLES - Accountability. - Transparency. - Privacy by
Design. - Auditability. - Continuous Improvement.

COMPLIANCE DOMAINS - Workflow governance. - Data protection. - Execution
logging. - Access control. - Regulatory reporting. - Policy enforcement.

COMPLIANCE PROCESS 1. Define compliance requirements. 2. Implement
workflow policies. 3. Monitor compliance status. 4. Perform regular
audits. 5. Correct non-compliance. 6. Improve compliance continuously.

QUALITY CONTROLS - Policy reviews. - Compliance reporting. - Audit
validation. - Risk assessments. - Control verification.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Workflow Engine Compliance shall ensure that all
workflow services operate in accordance with governance standards,
security policies and regulatory obligations throughout the GalSen IA
ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 07
