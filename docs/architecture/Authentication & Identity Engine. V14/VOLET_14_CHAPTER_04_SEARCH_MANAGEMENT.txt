GALSEN IA VOLET 14 - SEARCH ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 04 - SEARCH MANAGEMENT
Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the standards and operational processes for managing the
Search Engine across the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES - Standardize search management. - Ensure reliable search
services. - Maintain search quality. - Reduce operational risks. -
Support continuous improvement.

MANAGEMENT PRINCIPLES - Centralized governance. - Secure search
operations. - Consistent service delivery. - Continuous monitoring. -
Lifecycle accountability.

MANAGEMENT PROCESS 1. Register search sources. 2. Validate indexing
policies. 3. Configure search services. 4. Execute search operations. 5.
Monitor search performance. 6. Optimize indexes. 7. Retire obsolete
search assets.

OPERATIONAL CONTROLS - Configuration management. - Change management. -
Version control. - Incident tracking. - Performance monitoring.

QUALITY REQUIREMENTS - High availability. - Low query latency. -
Accurate search results. - Secure information access. - Complete
auditability.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Search management shall ensure that every search service
remains secure, reliable, maintainable and aligned with the strategic
objectives of the GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 04
