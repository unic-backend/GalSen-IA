GALSEN IA VOLET 14 - SEARCH ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 10 - SEARCH ENGINE
GOVERNANCE Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the enterprise governance model for the Search Engine to
ensure long-term sustainability, compliance and strategic alignment.

OBJECTIVES - Ensure enterprise-wide governance. - Maintain operational
excellence. - Support regulatory compliance. - Enable continuous
evolution. - Align search capabilities with business strategy.

GOVERNANCE FRAMEWORK - Strategic oversight. - Operational governance. -
Risk management. - Compliance management. - Performance management. -
Continuous improvement.

GOVERNANCE RESPONSIBILITIES - Define search strategy. - Approve major
changes. - Monitor KPIs. - Review security and compliance. - Manage
lifecycle evolution. - Coordinate cross-engine integration.

SUCCESS METRICS - Search availability. - Query performance. - Result
relevance. - Security compliance. - User satisfaction. - Continuous
improvement outcomes.

FINAL DIRECTIVE The Search Engine shall operate under a unified
governance framework that guarantees secure, reliable, scalable and
continuously improving search services throughout the GalSen IA
ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 10
