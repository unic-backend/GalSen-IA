GALSEN IA VOLET 14 - SEARCH ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 01 - SEARCH ENGINE
VISION Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the vision, mission and strategic objectives of the
Search Engine within the GalSen IA ecosystem.

VISION Provide fast, intelligent, secure and context-aware search
capabilities across all GalSen IA resources.

MISSION Enable users, agents and systems to efficiently discover
relevant information through accurate and scalable search services.

OBJECTIVES - Deliver fast search results. - Improve relevance and
accuracy. - Support semantic and keyword search. - Ensure secure access
to indexed content. - Enable enterprise scalability.

CORE PRINCIPLES - Accuracy. - Speed. - Security. - Relevance. -
Scalability. - Continuous improvement.

STRATEGIC CAPABILITIES - Full-text search. - Semantic search. -
Intelligent ranking. - Multi-source indexing. - Search analytics. -
Personalized search experiences.

FINAL DIRECTIVE The Search Engine shall provide secure, reliable and
intelligent search capabilities that improve knowledge discovery across
the GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 01
