GALSEN IA VOLET 9 - ANALYTICS ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 09 - ANALYTICS
QUALITY AND OPTIMIZATION Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the standards and continuous improvement processes that
ensure analytics quality, performance and scalability.

OBJECTIVES - Improve data quality. - Optimize analytics performance. -
Ensure reliable insights. - Reduce processing inefficiencies. - Support
continuous evolution.

QUALITY PRINCIPLES - Accuracy before speed. - Consistency. -
Completeness. - Measurability. - Continuous optimization.

QUALITY METRICS - Data accuracy - Processing latency - Report
reliability - Query performance - Resource utilization - User
satisfaction

OPTIMIZATION PROCESS 1. Measure performance. 2. Identify bottlenecks. 3.
Prioritize improvements. 4. Implement optimizations. 5. Validate
results. 6. Monitor long-term impact.

CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT - Audit analytics quality regularly. - Optimize
processing pipelines. - Improve reporting efficiency. - Eliminate
unnecessary complexity. - Adapt to evolving business needs.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Analytics quality and optimization shall ensure that the
Analytics Engine remains reliable, efficient, scalable and continuously
aligned with the strategic objectives of GalSen IA.
