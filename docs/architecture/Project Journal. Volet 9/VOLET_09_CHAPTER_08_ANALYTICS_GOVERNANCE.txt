GALSEN IA VOLET 9 - ANALYTICS ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 08 - ANALYTICS
GOVERNANCE Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the governance framework that manages the Analytics
Engine throughout its lifecycle.

OBJECTIVES - Ensure strategic alignment. - Maintain analytics quality. -
Protect security and compliance. - Establish accountability. - Drive
continuous improvement.

GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLES - Transparency. - Accountability. - Policy-driven
management. - Data stewardship. - Continuous review.

ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES - Analytics Governance Board. - Analytics
Engine Owner. - Data Stewards. - Operations Team. - Security Team. -
Compliance Team.

GOVERNANCE PROCESS 1. Define governance policies. 2. Review analytics
changes. 3. Approve deployments. 4. Monitor governance metrics. 5. Audit
compliance. 6. Continuously improve governance.

POLICIES - Maintain version control. - Enforce access controls. -
Preserve audit trails. - Review data quality regularly. - Retire
obsolete analytics assets safely.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Analytics governance shall ensure that all analytics
capabilities remain reliable, secure, traceable and aligned with the
long-term objectives of the GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 08
