GALSEN IA VOLET 9 - ANALYTICS ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 01 - ANALYTICS
ENGINE VISION Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the vision, mission and strategic role of the Analytics
Engine within the GalSen IA ecosystem.

VISION Build a scalable, intelligent and trustworthy analytics platform
that transforms data into actionable insights for users, AI systems and
business operations.

MISSION - Collect meaningful operational data. - Generate actionable
insights. - Support informed decision-making. - Measure system
performance. - Enable continuous improvement.

CORE PRINCIPLES - Data accuracy. - Transparency. - Privacy by design. -
Scalability. - Reliability. - Evidence-based decisions.

RESPONSIBILITIES - Collect analytics events. - Process operational
metrics. - Produce dashboards and reports. - Detect trends and
anomalies. - Support optimization initiatives.

SUCCESS CRITERIA - High data quality. - Reliable reporting. - Low
processing latency. - Actionable insights. - Continuous optimization.

FINAL DIRECTIVE The Analytics Engine serves as the intelligence layer
that transforms operational data into measurable knowledge, enabling
continuous improvement across the entire GalSen IA ecosystem.
