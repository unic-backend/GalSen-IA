GALSEN IA VOLET 9 - ANALYTICS ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 06 - ANALYTICS
REPORTING Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define how analytics results are transformed into reports and
dashboards that support operational and strategic decision-making.

OBJECTIVES - Deliver accurate reports. - Provide actionable insights. -
Support real-time and scheduled reporting. - Ensure report
consistency. - Protect sensitive information.

REPORT TYPES - Operational Reports - Performance Reports - Executive
Dashboards - AI Analytics Reports - Compliance Reports - Audit Reports

REPORTING PRINCIPLES - Accuracy. - Clarity. - Timeliness. -
Consistency. - Security.

REPORTING PROCESS 1. Retrieve validated analytics. 2. Generate metrics.
3. Build visual summaries. 4. Validate report quality. 5. Publish
securely. 6. Archive reports.

QUALITY CONTROLS - Data verification. - Report validation. - Access
control. - Version tracking. - Audit logging.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Analytics reporting shall provide reliable, secure and
understandable information that enables informed decisions throughout
the GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 06
