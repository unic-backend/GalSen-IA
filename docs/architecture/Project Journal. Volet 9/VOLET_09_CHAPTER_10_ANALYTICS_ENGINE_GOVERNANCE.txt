GALSEN IA VOLET 9 - ANALYTICS ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 10 - ANALYTICS
ENGINE GOVERNANCE Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the governance framework that oversees the Analytics
Engine throughout its lifecycle.

OBJECTIVES - Ensure strategic alignment. - Maintain analytics quality. -
Protect security and compliance. - Ensure accountability. - Support
continuous evolution.

GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLES - Transparency. - Accountability. - Security by
design. - Policy-driven management. - Continuous improvement.

ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES - Analytics Governance Board. - Analytics
Engine Owner. - Data Stewards. - Operations Team. - Security Team. -
Compliance Team. - System Architects.

GOVERNANCE PROCESS 1. Define governance policies. 2. Review major
analytics changes. 3. Assess operational risks. 4. Approve deployments.
5. Monitor governance metrics. 6. Audit compliance. 7. Continuously
improve governance.

AUDIT AND COMPLIANCE - Maintain audit trails. - Track governance KPIs. -
Review policy compliance. - Document governance decisions.

LONG-TERM EVOLUTION - Adapt governance to emerging technologies. -
Improve analytics standards. - Preserve interoperability. - Maintain
backward compatibility where practical.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Analytics Engine Governance shall ensure that every
analytics capability remains reliable, secure, traceable and aligned
with the long-term mission of GalSen IA.

END OF CHAPTER 10
