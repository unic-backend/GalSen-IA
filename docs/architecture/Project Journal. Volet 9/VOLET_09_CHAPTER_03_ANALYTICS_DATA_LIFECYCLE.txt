GALSEN IA VOLET 9 - ANALYTICS ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 03 - ANALYTICS DATA
LIFECYCLE Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the complete lifecycle of analytics data from collection
to archival within the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES - Ensure reliable data collection. - Maintain data quality. -
Support efficient processing. - Enable secure retention. - Improve
long-term analytics.

LIFECYCLE STAGES 1. Data Collection 2. Validation 3. Processing 4.
Enrichment 5. Storage 6. Analysis 7. Reporting 8. Archival 9. Deletion

LIFECYCLE PRINCIPLES - Data integrity. - Traceability. - Privacy by
design. - Governance compliance. - Continuous optimization.

DATA MANAGEMENT - Classify analytics data. - Preserve metadata. - Apply
retention policies. - Archive historical records. - Remove obsolete data
securely.

QUALITY CONTROLS - Completeness. - Accuracy. - Consistency. -
Timeliness. - Auditability.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Every analytics record shall follow a governed lifecycle
that ensures trustworthy insights, regulatory compliance and long-term
operational value across GalSen IA.

END OF CHAPTER 03
