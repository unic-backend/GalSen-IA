CHAPTER 08 - DECISION ENGINE GOVERNANCE

Version: 1.0
Language: English

PURPOSE

Define the governance framework for enterprise decision services, AI reasoning and decision intelligence across the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES

- Establish governance responsibilities.
- Ensure strategic alignment.
- Maintain security and compliance.
- Support accountability.
- Govern enterprise decision intelligence.
- Promote responsible AI.
- Drive continuous improvement.

GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLES

- Accountability.
- Transparency.
- Explainability.
- Policy-Driven Management.
- Responsible AI.
- Risk Awareness.
- Decision Integrity.
- Continuous Review.

ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

- Enterprise Decision Governance Board.
- Chief Decision Officer.
- AI Decision Architects.
- Business Rule Managers.
- Risk Management Team.
- Security Team.
- Compliance Officers.
- AI Ethics Committee.
- Audit Team.

GOVERNANCE DOMAINS

- Decision Governance.
- AI Governance.
- Business Rule Governance.
- Risk Governance.
- Explainability Governance.
- Access Governance.
- Compliance Governance.
- Performance Governance.
- Decision Lifecycle Governance.
- Quality Governance.

GOVERNANCE PROCESS

1. Define governance policies.
2. Approve enterprise decision standards.
3. Validate AI reasoning models.
4. Review business rules.
5. Monitor governance metrics.
6. Audit compliance.
7. Assess operational risks.
8. Validate decision quality.
9. Improve governance continuously.

QUALITY CONTROLS

- Governance reviews.
- Policy validation.
- Decision quality audits.
- AI explainability validation.
- Business rule verification.
- Risk assessments.
- Compliance monitoring.
- Continuous governance assessment.

SUCCESS METRICS

- Governance maturity.
- Decision quality score.
- Decision consistency index.
- AI explainability score.
- Policy compliance rate.
- Audit success rate.
- Operational efficiency.
- Continuous improvement index.

FINAL DIRECTIVE

Decision Engine Governance shall ensure secure, accountable, transparent and policy-driven governance of all enterprise decision services while maintaining trusted AI reasoning, business alignment and responsible decision intelligence throughout the GalSen IA ecosystem.
