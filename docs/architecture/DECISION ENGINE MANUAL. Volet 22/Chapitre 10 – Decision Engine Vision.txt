CHAPTER 10 - DECISION ENGINE GOVERNANCE

Version: 1.0
Language: English

PURPOSE

Define the enterprise governance model for the Decision Engine to ensure long-term sustainability, operational excellence, trusted AI reasoning and strategic alignment throughout the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES

- Ensure enterprise-wide decision governance.
- Maintain operational excellence.
- Support regulatory compliance.
- Enable continuous decision evolution.
- Align decision services with business strategy.
- Protect enterprise decision assets.
- Promote trusted, transparent and explainable AI decisions.

GOVERNANCE FRAMEWORK

- Strategic Oversight.
- Operational Governance.
- Decision Lifecycle Governance.
- AI Governance.
- Business Rule Governance.
- Risk Governance.
- Compliance Management.
- Performance Management.
- Explainability Governance.
- Continuous Improvement.

GOVERNANCE RESPONSIBILITIES

- Define enterprise decision strategy.
- Approve major decision architecture changes.
- Govern AI reasoning evolution.
- Monitor decision KPIs.
- Review security and compliance.
- Coordinate enterprise decision intelligence.
- Manage business rules.
- Drive continuous optimization.

SUCCESS METRICS

- Decision availability.
- Decision accuracy.
- Decision consistency.
- Explainability score.
- AI reasoning quality.
- Risk prediction accuracy.
- Security compliance.
- Operational efficiency.
- Business value creation.
- Continuous improvement index.

CONTINUOUS GOVERNANCE

- Continuous policy review.
- Decision quality assessments.
- AI reasoning reviews.
- Governance maturity assessments.
- Enterprise decision audits.
- Strategic roadmap reviews.
- Risk governance reviews.
- AI ethics assessments.

FINAL DIRECTIVE

The Decision Engine shall operate under a unified governance framework that guarantees secure, reliable, scalable, explainable and continuously improving enterprise decision services while preserving trusted AI reasoning, business alignment and strategic intelligence throughout the GalSen IA ecosystem.
