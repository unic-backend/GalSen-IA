GALSEN IA
VOLET 22 - DECISION ENGINE MANUAL
CHAPTER 02 - DECISION ENGINE ARCHITECTURE

Version: 1.0
Language: English

PURPOSE

Define the architecture of the Decision Engine and its integration across the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES

- Provide a modular decision architecture.
- Centralize enterprise decision services.
- Enable explainable AI reasoning.
- Ensure scalability and resilience.
- Support secure integrations.
- Simplify maintenance and future evolution.

ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS

- Decision Core.
- Decision Manager.
- Rule Engine.
- Inference Engine.
- Context Engine Connector.
- Memory Engine Connector.
- Knowledge Engine Connector.
- Risk Assessment Engine.
- Decision Repository.
- Decision Analytics Engine.
- Monitoring Service.

ARCHITECTURE PRINCIPLES

- Modular Architecture.
- Explainable AI.
- High Availability.
- Fault Tolerance.
- Secure Decision Processing.
- Context Awareness.
- Extensibility.

DECISION FLOW

1. Receive decision request.
2. Collect contextual information.
3. Retrieve relevant knowledge.
4. Evaluate business rules.
5. Execute AI reasoning.
6. Assess risks.
7. Generate decision.
8. Validate decision.
9. Record decision history.
10. Deliver explainable result.

QUALITY REQUIREMENTS

- High availability.
- Low decision latency.
- Explainability.
- Decision consistency.
- Scalability.
- Auditability.
- Performance monitoring.

FINAL DIRECTIVE

The Decision Engine architecture shall provide a secure, scalable, explainable and resilient foundation for enterprise decision-making throughout the GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 02
