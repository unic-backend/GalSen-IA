GALSEN IA
VOLET 22 - DECISION ENGINE MANUAL
CHAPTER 06 - DECISION ENGINE MONITORING

Version: 1.0
Language: English

PURPOSE

Define the monitoring framework for enterprise decision services, AI reasoning and decision intelligence across the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES

- Monitor decision operations.
- Detect anomalies and inconsistencies.
- Ensure decision service availability.
- Support incident investigation.
- Optimize decision performance.
- Enable continuous improvement.
- Maintain decision transparency.

MONITORING AREAS

- Decision execution.
- AI reasoning performance.
- Business rule evaluation.
- Decision repository health.
- Decision latency.
- Risk assessment accuracy.
- Decision consistency.
- Decision history.
- System health.
- Service availability.

MONITORING PRINCIPLES

- Real-time visibility.
- Continuous monitoring.
- Accuracy.
- Explainability.
- Traceability.
- Predictive analytics.
- Proactive alerting.

MONITORING PROCESS

1. Collect monitoring events.
2. Analyze decision metrics.
3. Detect anomalies.
4. Generate alerts.
5. Investigate incidents.
6. Execute corrective actions.
7. Validate recovery.
8. Improve monitoring policies.

KEY METRICS

- Decision success rate.
- Decision latency.
- AI reasoning time.
- Rule evaluation time.
- Decision accuracy.
- Risk prediction accuracy.
- Service uptime.
- Error rate.
- Recovery time.
- Explainability score.

MONITORING CONTROLS

- Health checks.
- Real-time dashboards.
- Centralized logging.
- Distributed tracing.
- Capacity monitoring.
- Performance profiling.
- SLA monitoring.
- Root cause analysis.
- Decision quality monitoring.

FINAL DIRECTIVE

Decision Engine Monitoring shall provide continuous visibility, intelligent analytics, proactive anomaly detection and complete operational traceability for all enterprise decision services throughout the GalSen IA ecosystem.
