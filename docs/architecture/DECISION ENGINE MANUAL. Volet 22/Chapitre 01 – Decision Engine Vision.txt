GALSEN IA
VOLET 22 - DECISION ENGINE MANUAL
CHAPTER 01 - DECISION ENGINE VISION

Version: 1.0
Language: English

PURPOSE

Define the vision, mission and strategic objectives of the Decision Engine within the GalSen IA ecosystem.

VISION

Provide an intelligent, explainable and autonomous decision platform capable of transforming knowledge, context and reasoning into reliable enterprise decisions across the entire GalSen IA ecosystem.

MISSION

Deliver secure, transparent and scalable decision-making capabilities that empower AI agents, workflows and enterprise services to make accurate, contextual and data-driven decisions.

OBJECTIVES

- Centralize enterprise decision management.
- Enable intelligent decision making.
- Support contextual reasoning.
- Ensure explainable AI decisions.
- Reduce operational uncertainty.
- Improve business efficiency.
- Enable continuous decision optimization.
- Ensure scalability and reliability.

CORE PRINCIPLES

- Intelligence.
- Explainability.
- Reliability.
- Transparency.
- Context Awareness.
- Scalability.
- Accountability.
- Continuous Improvement.

STRATEGIC CAPABILITIES

- Decision Management.
- Rule-Based Decision Making.
- AI Decision Reasoning.
- Decision Trees.
- Risk Evaluation.
- Contextual Decision Support.
- Decision Analytics.
- Decision Optimization.
- Multi-Agent Decision Coordination.
- Human-in-the-Loop Decision Support.

DECISION SOURCES

- Enterprise Knowledge.
- Business Rules.
- AI Models.
- Context Engine.
- Memory Engine.
- Knowledge Engine.
- External APIs.
- User Preferences.
- Historical Decisions.

FINAL DIRECTIVE

The Decision Engine shall provide secure, scalable, intelligent and explainable enterprise decision services that become the trusted decision-making foundation for the entire GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 01
