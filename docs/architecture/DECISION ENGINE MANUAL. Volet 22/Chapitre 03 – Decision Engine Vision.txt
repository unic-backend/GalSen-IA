VOLET 22 - DECISION ENGINE MANUAL
CHAPTER 03 - DECISION ENGINE LIFECYCLE

Version: 1.0
Language: English

PURPOSE

Define the complete lifecycle of enterprise decisions from request to execution, evaluation and retirement across the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES

- Standardize decision operations.
- Ensure reliable decision management.
- Preserve decision consistency.
- Support explainable AI.
- Enable continuous learning.
- Maintain complete traceability.

LIFECYCLE STAGES

1. Decision Request.
2. Context Collection.
3. Knowledge Retrieval.
4. Decision Analysis.
5. Rule Evaluation.
6. AI Reasoning.
7. Risk Assessment.
8. Decision Validation.
9. Decision Execution.
10. Decision Recording.
11. Decision Evaluation.
12. Decision Optimization.
13. Decision Archival.
14. Decision Retirement.

LIFECYCLE PRINCIPLES

- Reliability.
- Explainability.
- Transparency.
- Accountability.
- Context Awareness.
- Security.
- Scalability.
- Continuous Learning.

MANAGEMENT PRACTICES

- Validate every decision request.
- Collect complete contextual information.
- Evaluate enterprise policies.
- Record every decision.
- Monitor decision quality.
- Learn from historical decisions.
- Archive obsolete decisions securely.
- Continuously improve decision models.

QUALITY CONTROLS

- Decision integrity validation.
- Rule consistency verification.
- AI reasoning validation.
- Risk assessment verification.
- Audit logging.
- Performance monitoring.
- Explainability assessment.

LIFECYCLE METRICS

- Decision success rate.
- Decision latency.
- Decision accuracy.
- Risk prediction accuracy.
- Explainability score.
- Decision consistency.
- Decision reuse rate.
- Continuous improvement index.

FINAL DIRECTIVE

Every enterprise decision shall follow a governed lifecycle that guarantees secure, reliable, explainable and continuously improving decision intelligence throughout the GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 03
