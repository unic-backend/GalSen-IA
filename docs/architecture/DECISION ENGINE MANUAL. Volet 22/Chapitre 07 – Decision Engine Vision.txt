GALSEN IA
VOLET 22 - DECISION ENGINE MANUAL
CHAPTER 07 - DECISION ENGINE COMPLIANCE

Version: 1.0
Language: English

PURPOSE

Define the compliance framework for enterprise decision services, AI reasoning and decision intelligence across the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES

- Ensure regulatory compliance.
- Protect enterprise decision assets.
- Maintain audit readiness.
- Enforce governance policies.
- Preserve transparency and accountability.
- Guarantee decision integrity.
- Support continuous compliance improvement.

COMPLIANCE PRINCIPLES

- Accountability.
- Transparency.
- Explainability.
- Privacy by Design.
- Auditability.
- Responsible AI.
- Decision Integrity.
- Continuous Improvement.

COMPLIANCE DOMAINS

- Decision Governance.
- AI Governance.
- Business Rule Compliance.
- Decision Audit.
- Data Protection.
- Access Control.
- Regulatory Reporting.
- Privacy Management.
- Risk Management.
- Ethical AI Compliance.

COMPLIANCE PROCESS

1. Define compliance requirements.
2. Classify decision assets.
3. Apply governance policies.
4. Validate AI reasoning.
5. Monitor compliance status.
6. Perform periodic audits.
7. Correct non-compliance.
8. Validate compliance evidence.
9. Improve compliance continuously.

QUALITY CONTROLS

- Policy validation.
- Decision audit.
- Compliance reporting.
- Risk assessments.
- Privacy impact assessments.
- AI ethics reviews.
- Governance verification.
- Control effectiveness evaluation.

COMPLIANCE METRICS

- Compliance score.
- Audit success rate.
- Policy violation count.
- Decision integrity score.
- AI explainability score.
- Privacy incident rate.
- Regulatory conformity.
- Governance maturity.

FINAL DIRECTIVE

Decision Engine Compliance shall ensure that all enterprise decision services, AI reasoning processes and decision intelligence operate in accordance with governance standards, security policies, responsible AI principles and regulatory obligations throughout the GalSen IA ecosystem.
