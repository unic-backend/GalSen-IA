GALSEN IA
VOLET 22 - DECISION ENGINE MANUAL
CHAPTER 04 - DECISION ENGINE MANAGEMENT

Version: 1.0
Language: English

PURPOSE

Define the management framework for enterprise decision services, decision intelligence and AI reasoning across the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES

- Centralize enterprise decision administration.
- Manage decision repositories.
- Control the complete decision lifecycle.
- Ensure operational reliability.
- Support governance policies.
- Preserve decision consistency.
- Maximize decision quality.
- Enable continuous optimization.

MANAGEMENT DOMAINS

- Decision Repository Management.
- Decision Rule Management.
- AI Reasoning Management.
- Context Management.
- Risk Management.
- Decision History Management.
- Decision Analytics.
- Decision Audit.
- Decision Lifecycle Management.

MANAGEMENT PRINCIPLES

- Reliability.
- Scalability.
- Explainability.
- Accountability.
- Transparency.
- Security.
- Continuous Monitoring.
- Decision Integrity.

OPERATIONAL PROCESS

1. Register decision requests.
2. Validate business rules.
3. Retrieve contextual information.
4. Execute AI reasoning.
5. Assess risks.
6. Validate decision outputs.
7. Record decision history.
8. Monitor decision quality.
9. Optimize decision models.
10. Archive completed decision records.

QUALITY CONTROLS

- Decision validation.
- Rule consistency verification.
- AI reasoning verification.
- Context validation.
- Risk assessment verification.
- Audit logging.
- Performance monitoring.
- Decision quality assessment.

MANAGEMENT METRICS

- Decision availability.
- Decision accuracy.
- Decision latency.
- Decision consistency.
- Risk prediction accuracy.
- Explainability score.
- Operational efficiency.
- Decision reuse rate.

FINAL DIRECTIVE

Decision Engine Management shall ensure secure, centralized, scalable and auditable management of all enterprise decision services while maintaining explainability, consistency and intelligent decision-making throughout the GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 04
