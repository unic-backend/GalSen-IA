GALSEN IA VOLET 16 - AUTHENTICATION & IDENTITY ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER
04 - AUTHENTICATION & IDENTITY MANAGEMENT Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the management framework for identities, credentials and
authentication services across the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES - Centralize identity administration. - Manage user and
service accounts. - Control credentials securely. - Maintain identity
integrity. - Support lifecycle governance.

MANAGEMENT DOMAINS - User Identity Management. - Service Identity
Management. - Role Management. - Credential Management. - Session
Management. - Identity Audit.

MANAGEMENT PRINCIPLES - Least Privilege. - Zero Trust. - Strong
Authentication. - Accountability. - Continuous Monitoring.

OPERATIONAL PROCESS 1. Create identity. 2. Assign roles. 3. Configure
authentication methods. 4. Monitor access activity. 5. Update identities
securely. 6. Disable or retire obsolete identities.

QUALITY CONTROLS - Identity validation. - Credential rotation. - Access
reviews. - Audit logging. - Compliance verification.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Authentication and Identity Management shall ensure
secure, centralized and auditable control of all identities and access
privileges throughout the GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 04
