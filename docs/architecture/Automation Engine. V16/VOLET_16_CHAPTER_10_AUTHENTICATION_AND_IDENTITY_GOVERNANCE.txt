GALSEN IA VOLET 16 - AUTHENTICATION & IDENTITY ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER
10 - AUTHENTICATION & IDENTITY GOVERNANCE Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the enterprise governance model for the Authentication &
Identity Engine to ensure long-term sustainability, security and
strategic alignment.

OBJECTIVES - Ensure enterprise-wide identity governance. - Maintain
operational excellence. - Support regulatory compliance. - Enable
continuous evolution. - Align identity services with business strategy.

GOVERNANCE FRAMEWORK - Strategic oversight. - Operational governance. -
Risk management. - Compliance management. - Identity lifecycle
governance. - Continuous improvement.

GOVERNANCE RESPONSIBILITIES - Define identity strategy. - Approve major
architectural changes. - Monitor security and compliance KPIs. - Review
authentication policies. - Coordinate cross-system integration. - Drive
continuous optimization.

SUCCESS METRICS - Authentication availability. - Identity service
reliability. - Security compliance. - Operational efficiency. - User
trust. - Continuous improvement outcomes.

FINAL DIRECTIVE The Authentication & Identity Engine shall operate under
a unified governance framework that guarantees secure, reliable,
scalable and continuously improving identity services throughout the
GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 10
