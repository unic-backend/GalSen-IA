GALSEN IA VOLET 16 - AUTHENTICATION & IDENTITY ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER
01 - AUTHENTICATION & IDENTITY VISION Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the vision, mission and strategic objectives of the
Authentication & Identity Engine within the GalSen IA ecosystem.

VISION Provide secure, intelligent and unified identity management for
users, agents, services and devices across the GalSen IA ecosystem.

MISSION Deliver trusted authentication, identity management and access
control services that protect all digital resources.

OBJECTIVES - Secure user authentication. - Centralize identity
management. - Enable fine-grained authorization. - Support regulatory
compliance. - Ensure scalability and reliability.

CORE PRINCIPLES - Security. - Privacy. - Trust. - Scalability. -
Interoperability. - Continuous improvement.

STRATEGIC CAPABILITIES - Identity management. - Authentication
services. - Authorization policies. - Single Sign-On. - Multi-Factor
Authentication. - Identity analytics.

FINAL DIRECTIVE The Authentication & Identity Engine shall provide
secure, reliable and unified identity services across the entire GalSen
IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 01
