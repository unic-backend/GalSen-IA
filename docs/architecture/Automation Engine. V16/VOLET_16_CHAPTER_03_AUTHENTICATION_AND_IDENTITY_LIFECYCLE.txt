GALSEN IA VOLET 16 - AUTHENTICATION & IDENTITY ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER
03 - AUTHENTICATION & IDENTITY LIFECYCLE Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the complete lifecycle of authentication and identity
operations from identity registration to secure retirement within the
GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES - Standardize identity operations. - Ensure secure
authentication. - Maintain traceability. - Protect identity data. -
Enable continuous improvement.

LIFECYCLE STAGES 1. Identity Registration 2. Identity Verification 3.
Authentication 4. Authorization 5. Token Issuance 6. Access Monitoring
7. Identity Update 8. Identity Suspension or Revocation 9. Secure
Retirement and Archival

LIFECYCLE PRINCIPLES - Security. - Privacy. - Reliability. -
Traceability. - Scalability.

MANAGEMENT PRACTICES - Verify identities before activation. - Enforce
access policies. - Monitor authentication events. - Audit identity
changes. - Remove obsolete identities securely.

QUALITY CONTROLS - Identity verification. - Authentication validation. -
Audit logging. - Access monitoring. - Performance monitoring.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Every identity and authentication operation shall follow
a governed lifecycle that guarantees secure, reliable and traceable
access throughout the GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 03
