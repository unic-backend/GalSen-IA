GALSEN IA VOLET 16 - AUTHENTICATION & IDENTITY ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER
07 - AUTHENTICATION & IDENTITY COMPLIANCE Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the compliance framework for authentication, identity
management and access control across the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES - Ensure regulatory compliance. - Enforce identity
governance. - Maintain audit readiness. - Protect sensitive identity
data. - Support continuous compliance improvement.

COMPLIANCE PRINCIPLES - Accountability. - Privacy by Design. - Least
Privilege. - Auditability. - Transparency.

COMPLIANCE DOMAINS - Identity governance. - Access control. -
Authentication policies. - Audit logging. - Data protection. -
Regulatory reporting.

COMPLIANCE PROCESS 1. Define compliance requirements. 2. Implement
security controls. 3. Monitor compliance status. 4. Perform regular
audits. 5. Correct non-compliance. 6. Improve compliance continuously.

QUALITY CONTROLS - Policy reviews. - Access certification. - Audit
validation. - Compliance reporting. - Risk assessments.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Authentication and Identity Compliance shall ensure that
all identity services operate in accordance with security policies,
regulatory obligations and governance standards throughout the GalSen IA
ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 07
