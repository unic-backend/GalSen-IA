PURPOSE Define the quality assurance and continuous optimization
framework for authentication and identity services across the GalSen IA
ecosystem.

service performance. - Reduce operational risks. - Enhance user
experience. - Enable continuous improvement.

QUALITY PRINCIPLES - Reliability. - Performance. - Security. -
Consistency. - Continuous optimization.

OPTIMIZATION PROCESS 1. Measure service performance. 2. Analyze quality
metrics. 3. Identify improvement opportunities. 4. Implement
optimizations. 5. Validate outcomes. 6. Repeat continuously.

KEY METRICS - Authentication success rate. - Authentication latency. -
Identity service availability. - Failed login rate. - Incident
resolution time. - User satisfaction.

QUALITY CONTROLS - Continuous testing. - Performance benchmarking. -
Capacity planning. - Root cause analysis. - Periodic quality reviews.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Authentication and Identity Quality and Optimization
shall continuously improve the reliability, security, scalability and
efficiency of identity services throughout the GalSen IA ecosystem.
