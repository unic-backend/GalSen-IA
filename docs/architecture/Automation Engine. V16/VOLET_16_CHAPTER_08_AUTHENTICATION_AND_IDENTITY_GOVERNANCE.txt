GALSEN IA VOLET 16 - AUTHENTICATION & IDENTITY ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER
08 - AUTHENTICATION & IDENTITY GOVERNANCE Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the governance framework for authentication, identity
management and access control across the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES - Establish governance responsibilities. - Ensure strategic
alignment. - Maintain security and compliance. - Support
accountability. - Drive continuous improvement.

GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLES - Accountability. - Transparency. - Policy-driven
management. - Risk awareness. - Continuous review.

ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES - Identity Governance Board. - Security
Team. - Identity Administrators. - Compliance Officers. - System
Owners. - Audit Team.

GOVERNANCE PROCESS 1. Define identity policies. 2. Review governance
objectives. 3. Approve major changes. 4. Monitor governance metrics. 5.
Audit compliance. 6. Continuously improve governance.

QUALITY CONTROLS - Governance reviews. - Policy validation. - Audit
reporting. - Risk assessments. - Continuous monitoring.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Authentication and Identity Governance shall ensure
secure, accountable and policy-driven identity services across the
entire GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 08
