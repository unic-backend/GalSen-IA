GALSEN IA VOLET 5 - KNOWLEDGE ENGINE CHAPTER 04 - KNOWLEDGE VALIDATION
Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the standards for validating knowledge before it becomes
part of the GalSen IA Knowledge Engine.

OBJECTIVES - Ensure accuracy. - Prevent misinformation. - Maintain
consistency. - Increase trust. - Support reliable AI reasoning.

VALIDATION PRINCIPLES - Verify information using trusted sources. -
Confirm factual accuracy. - Check consistency with existing knowledge. -
Identify conflicts and duplicates. - Record validation status.

VALIDATION LEVELS - Draft - Under Review - Verified - Approved -
Deprecated

QUALITY CHECKS - Source credibility - Technical correctness - Business
relevance - Completeness - Clarity - Version consistency

REVIEW PROCESS 1. Initial validation 2. Peer review 3. Expert approval
(when required) 4. Publication 5. Periodic revalidation

GOVERNANCE - Record validator identity or role. - Preserve validation
history. - Revalidate after significant changes. - Archive superseded
knowledge.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Only validated knowledge should be considered
authoritative for AI reasoning and decision support.

END OF CHAPTER 04
