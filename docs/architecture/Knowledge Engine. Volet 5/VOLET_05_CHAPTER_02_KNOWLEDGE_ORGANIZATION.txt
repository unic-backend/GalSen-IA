GALSEN IA VOLET 5 - KNOWLEDGE ENGINE CHAPTER 02 - KNOWLEDGE ORGANIZATION
Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define how knowledge is organized to ensure consistency,
discoverability and long-term maintainability.

OBJECTIVES - Organize information logically. - Reduce duplication. -
Improve retrieval accuracy. - Simplify maintenance. - Support future
expansion.

ORGANIZATION PRINCIPLES - Use clear categories. - Group related topics
together. - Assign metadata to every knowledge item. - Maintain explicit
relationships between documents. - Archive obsolete information without
losing history.

STRUCTURE - Domains - Categories - Topics - Documents - Tags -
References - Versions

CLASSIFICATION Knowledge should be classified by: - Source -
Reliability - Sensitivity - Audience - Status (Draft, Reviewed,
Approved, Archived)

MAINTENANCE - Review content regularly. - Merge duplicate information. -
Preserve version history. - Record ownership for each knowledge area.

FINAL DIRECTIVE A well-organized knowledge base enables reliable AI
reasoning, efficient search and sustainable long-term growth.

END OF CHAPTER 02
