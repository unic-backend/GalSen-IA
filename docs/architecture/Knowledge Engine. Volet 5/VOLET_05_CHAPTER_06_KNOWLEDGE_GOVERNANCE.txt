GALSEN IA VOLET 5 - KNOWLEDGE ENGINE CHAPTER 06 - KNOWLEDGE GOVERNANCE
Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the governance model that ensures knowledge remains
accurate, secure, consistent and sustainable across GalSen IA.

OBJECTIVES - Establish clear ownership. - Maintain knowledge quality. -
Protect sensitive information. - Ensure accountability. - Support
continuous improvement.

GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLES - Assign an owner to every knowledge domain. -
Define review responsibilities. - Preserve auditability. - Apply
consistent policies. - Promote transparency.

ROLES - Knowledge Owner - Knowledge Reviewer - Knowledge Contributor -
System Administrator - AI Orchestrator (consumer only)

POLICIES - Approve important changes before publication. - Track all
significant revisions. - Archive superseded content. - Periodically
review critical knowledge. - Enforce access permissions.

COMPLIANCE - Respect data governance policies. - Preserve version
history. - Protect confidential information. - Support regulatory
requirements where applicable.

CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT - Measure knowledge quality. - Identify outdated
content. - Improve governance processes. - Refine standards over time.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Knowledge governance must ensure that the Knowledge
Engine remains trustworthy, secure and sustainable throughout the
lifetime of GalSen IA.

END OF CHAPTER 06
