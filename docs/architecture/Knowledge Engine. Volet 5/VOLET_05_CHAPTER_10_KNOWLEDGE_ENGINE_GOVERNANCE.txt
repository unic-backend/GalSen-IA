GALSEN IA VOLET 5 - KNOWLEDGE ENGINE CHAPTER 10 - KNOWLEDGE ENGINE
GOVERNANCE Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the governance framework that oversees the entire
Knowledge Engine throughout its lifecycle.

OBJECTIVES - Ensure strategic alignment. - Maintain long-term knowledge
quality. - Protect integrity and security. - Support accountability. -
Enable continuous evolution.

GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLES - Clear ownership. - Transparent
decision-making. - Documented policies. - Risk-aware management. -
Continuous improvement.

RESPONSIBILITIES - Define governance policies. - Approve major knowledge
changes. - Monitor quality metrics. - Review security and compliance. -
Coordinate cross-domain knowledge management.

DECISION PROCESS 1. Identify proposed change. 2. Assess impact and
risks. 3. Review by responsible stakeholders. 4. Approve or reject. 5.
Record the decision. 6. Monitor outcomes.

AUDIT AND REPORTING - Maintain complete audit trails. - Publish
governance metrics. - Review governance effectiveness regularly. - Track
policy compliance.

LONG-TERM EVOLUTION - Periodically update governance standards. - Adapt
to new technologies. - Improve processes using lessons learned. -
Preserve backward compatibility where appropriate.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Knowledge Engine governance shall ensure that every
knowledge asset remains reliable, secure, traceable and aligned with the
mission of GalSen IA.

END OF CHAPTER 10
