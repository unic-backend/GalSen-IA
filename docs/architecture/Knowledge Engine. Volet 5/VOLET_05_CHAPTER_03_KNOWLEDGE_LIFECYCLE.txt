GALSEN IA VOLET 5 - KNOWLEDGE ENGINE CHAPTER 03 - KNOWLEDGE LIFECYCLE
Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the complete lifecycle for creating, validating,
maintaining and retiring knowledge within GalSen IA.

OBJECTIVES - Ensure knowledge quality. - Maintain accuracy over time. -
Support controlled evolution. - Preserve historical information. -
Enable continuous improvement.

LIFECYCLE STAGES 1. Creation 2. Review 3. Validation 4. Approval 5.
Publication 6. Maintenance 7. Archiving 8. Retirement

CREATION RULES - Use trusted sources. - Define ownership. - Add
metadata. - Classify the content. - Avoid duplicate entries.

REVIEW AND APPROVAL - Verify accuracy. - Check consistency. - Confirm
relevance. - Approve before publication. - Record review history.

MAINTENANCE - Review periodically. - Update outdated information. -
Preserve previous versions. - Archive obsolete knowledge responsibly.

GOVERNANCE - Assign clear ownership. - Maintain auditability. - Track
revisions. - Document major changes.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Knowledge is a living asset and must evolve through a
disciplined lifecycle that preserves quality, traceability and long-term
value.

END OF CHAPTER 03
