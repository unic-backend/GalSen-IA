GALSEN IA VOLET 5 - KNOWLEDGE ENGINE CHAPTER 09 - KNOWLEDGE QUALITY AND
CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the standards used to measure, improve and sustain the
quality of knowledge throughout the lifetime of GalSen IA.

OBJECTIVES - Maintain accurate knowledge. - Improve reliability over
time. - Eliminate obsolete information. - Encourage continuous
refinement. - Support trustworthy AI reasoning.

QUALITY PRINCIPLES - Accuracy before quantity. - Consistency across all
domains. - Clear ownership. - Measurable quality. - Continuous review.

QUALITY METRICS - Accuracy rate - Completeness - Freshness - Duplicate
rate - Validation coverage - User feedback

IMPROVEMENT PROCESS 1. Monitor quality metrics. 2. Identify gaps. 3.
Prioritize improvements. 4. Update knowledge. 5. Validate changes. 6.
Measure results.

CONTINUOUS REVIEW - Schedule periodic audits. - Archive outdated
content. - Merge duplicate entries. - Improve metadata quality. - Refine
search relevance.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Knowledge quality is a continuous responsibility. Every
improvement should increase trust, usability and long-term value across
the GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 09
