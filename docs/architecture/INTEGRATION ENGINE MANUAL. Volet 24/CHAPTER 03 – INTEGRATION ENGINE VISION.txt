VOLET 24 - INTEGRATION ENGINE MANUAL
CHAPTER 03 - INTEGRATION ENGINE LIFECYCLE

Version: 1.0
Language: English

PURPOSE

Define the lifecycle governing enterprise integrations.

OBJECTIVES

- Standardize integrations.
- Ensure reliability.
- Support continuous evolution.
- Preserve interoperability.

LIFECYCLE STAGES

1. Integration Request.
2. Validation.
3. Authentication.
4. Configuration.
5. Deployment.
6. Execution.
7. Monitoring.
8. Optimization.
9. Versioning.
10. Retirement.

LIFECYCLE PRINCIPLES

- Reliability.
- Security.
- Traceability.
- Scalability.
- Continuous Improvement.

QUALITY CONTROLS

- Validation.
- Integration testing.
- Audit logging.
- Performance monitoring.
- Error tracking.

FINAL DIRECTIVE

Every enterprise integration shall follow a governed lifecycle ensuring secure and reliable interoperability.

END OF CHAPTER 03
