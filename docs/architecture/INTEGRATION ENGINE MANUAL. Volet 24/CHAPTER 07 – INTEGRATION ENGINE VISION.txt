VOLET 24 - INTEGRATION ENGINE MANUAL
CHAPTER 07 - INTEGRATION ENGINE COMPLIANCE

Version: 1.0
Language: English

PURPOSE

Define the compliance framework governing enterprise integrations and external communications.

OBJECTIVES

- Ensure regulatory compliance.
- Protect enterprise data.
- Maintain audit readiness.
- Enforce governance policies.
- Preserve interoperability standards.
- Support continuous compliance improvement.

COMPLIANCE PRINCIPLES

- Accountability.
- Transparency.
- Auditability.
- Privacy by Design.
- Responsible Data Exchange.
- Continuous Improvement.

COMPLIANCE DOMAINS

- API Governance.
- Data Protection.
- Access Control.
- Audit Logging.
- Regulatory Reporting.
- Privacy Management.
- Connector Compliance.
- Security Compliance.

QUALITY CONTROLS

- Compliance audits.
- Policy validation.
- API verification.
- Connector certification.
- Risk assessments.
- Governance monitoring.

FINAL DIRECTIVE

Integration Engine Compliance shall ensure that every enterprise integration complies with security, governance and regulatory requirements.

END OF CHAPTER 07
