GALSEN IA
VOLET 21 - KNOWLEDGE ENGINE MANUAL
CHAPTER 03 - KNOWLEDGE ENGINE LIFECYCLE

Version: 1.0
Language: English

PURPOSE

Define the complete lifecycle of enterprise knowledge from acquisition to retirement across the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES

- Standardize knowledge operations.
- Ensure reliable knowledge management.
- Preserve semantic consistency.
- Enable continuous knowledge evolution.
- Support intelligent reasoning.
- Maintain full traceability.

LIFECYCLE STAGES

1. Knowledge Acquisition.
2. Knowledge Validation.
3. Knowledge Classification.
4. Semantic Enrichment.
5. Knowledge Storage.
6. Knowledge Indexing.
7. Knowledge Retrieval.
8. Knowledge Sharing.
9. Knowledge Synchronization.
10. Knowledge Optimization.
11. Knowledge Archival.
12. Secure Knowledge Retirement.

LIFECYCLE PRINCIPLES

- Reliability.
- Semantic Consistency.
- Context Awareness.
- Security.
- Scalability.
- Traceability.
- Continuous Learning.
- Knowledge Evolution.

MANAGEMENT PRACTICES

- Validate every knowledge asset.
- Remove duplicate knowledge.
- Maintain semantic relationships.
- Synchronize distributed repositories.
- Continuously improve knowledge quality.
- Archive inactive knowledge securely.
- Retire obsolete knowledge according to governance policies.

QUALITY CONTROLS

- Knowledge integrity validation.
- Semantic consistency verification.
- Duplicate detection.
- Retrieval accuracy testing.
- Audit logging.
- Performance monitoring.
- Knowledge quality assessment.

LIFECYCLE METRICS

- Knowledge acquisition rate.
- Validation success rate.
- Retrieval accuracy.
- Semantic consistency score.
- Duplicate reduction rate.
- Repository growth.
- Knowledge reuse rate.
- Knowledge freshness index.

FINAL DIRECTIVE

Every knowledge asset shall follow a governed lifecycle that guarantees secure, reliable, contextual and continuously evolving enterprise knowledge management throughout the GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 03
