VOLET 21 - KNOWLEDGE ENGINE MANUAL
CHAPTER 08 - KNOWLEDGE ENGINE GOVERNANCE

Version: 1.0
Language: English

PURPOSE

Define the governance framework for enterprise knowledge, semantic repositories and knowledge intelligence across the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES

- Establish governance responsibilities.
- Ensure strategic alignment.
- Maintain security and compliance.
- Support accountability.
- Protect enterprise knowledge.
- Govern semantic intelligence.
- Drive continuous improvement.

GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLES

- Accountability.
- Transparency.
- Policy-Driven Management.
- Knowledge Integrity.
- Semantic Consistency.
- Responsible AI.
- Risk Awareness.
- Continuous Review.

ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

- Enterprise Knowledge Governance Board.
- Chief Knowledge Officer.
- Knowledge Architects.
- Ontology Administrators.
- Data Stewards.
- AI System Owners.
- Security Team.
- Compliance Officers.
- Audit Team.

GOVERNANCE DOMAINS

- Enterprise Knowledge Governance.
- Knowledge Graph Governance.
- Ontology Governance.
- Metadata Governance.
- Semantic Governance.
- Access Governance.
- Retention Governance.
- Privacy Governance.
- AI Knowledge Governance.
- Quality Governance.

GOVERNANCE PROCESS

1. Define governance policies.
2. Approve enterprise knowledge standards.
3. Classify knowledge assets.
4. Review semantic models.
5. Monitor governance metrics.
6. Audit compliance.
7. Assess operational risks.
8. Validate knowledge quality.
9. Improve governance continuously.

QUALITY CONTROLS

- Governance reviews.
- Policy validation.
- Knowledge quality audits.
- Metadata verification.
- Ontology validation.
- Risk assessments.
- Compliance monitoring.
- Continuous governance assessment.

SUCCESS METRICS

- Governance maturity.
- Knowledge quality score.
- Semantic consistency index.
- Policy compliance rate.
- Audit success rate.
- Metadata quality score.
- Enterprise knowledge reuse rate.
- Continuous improvement index.

FINAL DIRECTIVE

Knowledge Engine Governance shall ensure secure, accountable, policy-driven and continuously improving governance of all enterprise knowledge, semantic intelligence and knowledge assets while supporting trusted AI operations throughout the GalSen IA ecosystem.
