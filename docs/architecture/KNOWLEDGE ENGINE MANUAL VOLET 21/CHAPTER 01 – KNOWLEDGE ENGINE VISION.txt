GALSEN IA
VOLET 21 - KNOWLEDGE ENGINE MANUAL
CHAPTER 01 - KNOWLEDGE ENGINE VISION

Version: 1.0
Language: English

PURPOSE

Define the vision, mission and strategic objectives of the Knowledge Engine within the GalSen IA ecosystem.

VISION

Provide a unified, intelligent and continuously evolving knowledge platform that transforms data, information and experience into actionable enterprise knowledge for every AI agent, workflow and decision process.

MISSION

Deliver centralized knowledge management, semantic understanding and knowledge reasoning capabilities that enable all GalSen IA services to access reliable, contextual and continuously enriched knowledge.

OBJECTIVES

- Centralize enterprise knowledge.
- Build a unified knowledge graph.
- Enable semantic understanding.
- Support intelligent reasoning.
- Improve AI decision quality.
- Enable continuous knowledge evolution.
- Ensure scalability and reliability.

CORE PRINCIPLES

- Knowledge First.
- Semantic Intelligence.
- Accuracy.
- Reliability.
- Context Awareness.
- Scalability.
- Explainability.
- Continuous Improvement.

STRATEGIC CAPABILITIES

- Knowledge Repository.
- Knowledge Graph.
- Ontology Management.
- Semantic Search.
- Knowledge Discovery.
- Knowledge Validation.
- Knowledge Evolution.
- Knowledge Analytics.
- Knowledge Sharing.
- Cross-Agent Knowledge Access.

KNOWLEDGE SOURCES

- Internal databases.
- Enterprise documents.
- AI-generated knowledge.
- Human expertise.
- External APIs.
- Structured data.
- Unstructured data.
- Historical knowledge.

FINAL DIRECTIVE

The Knowledge Engine shall provide secure, scalable, intelligent and continuously evolving enterprise knowledge services that become the single trusted knowledge foundation for the entire GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 01
