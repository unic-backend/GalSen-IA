CHAPTER 10 - KNOWLEDGE ENGINE GOVERNANCE

Version: 1.0
Language: English

PURPOSE

Define the enterprise governance model for the Knowledge Engine to ensure long-term sustainability, operational excellence, semantic integrity and strategic alignment throughout the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES

- Ensure enterprise-wide knowledge governance.
- Maintain operational excellence.
- Support regulatory compliance.
- Enable continuous knowledge evolution.
- Align knowledge services with business strategy.
- Protect enterprise knowledge assets.
- Promote trusted and explainable AI knowledge.

GOVERNANCE FRAMEWORK

- Strategic Oversight.
- Operational Governance.
- Knowledge Lifecycle Governance.
- Ontology Governance.
- Semantic Governance.
- Metadata Governance.
- Risk Management.
- Compliance Management.
- Performance Management.
- Continuous Improvement.

GOVERNANCE RESPONSIBILITIES

- Define enterprise knowledge strategy.
- Approve major knowledge architecture changes.
- Govern ontology evolution.
- Monitor knowledge KPIs.
- Review security and compliance.
- Coordinate enterprise knowledge sharing.
- Manage semantic standards.
- Drive continuous optimization.

SUCCESS METRICS

- Knowledge availability.
- Knowledge integrity.
- Semantic consistency.
- Retrieval accuracy.
- Knowledge reuse rate.
- Ontology quality.
- Security compliance.
- Operational efficiency.
- Explainability score.
- Continuous improvement index.

CONTINUOUS GOVERNANCE

- Continuous policy review.
- Knowledge quality assessments.
- Ontology evolution reviews.
- Governance maturity assessments.
- Enterprise knowledge audits.
- Strategic roadmap reviews.
- Semantic optimization reviews.
- AI knowledge governance assessments.

FINAL DIRECTIVE

The Knowledge Engine shall operate under a unified governance framework that guarantees secure, reliable, scalable, explainable and continuously improving enterprise knowledge services while preserving semantic integrity, organizational intelligence and trusted AI reasoning throughout the GalSen IA ecosystem.
