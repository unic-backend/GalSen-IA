GALSEN IA
VOLET 21 - KNOWLEDGE ENGINE MANUAL
CHAPTER 04 - KNOWLEDGE ENGINE MANAGEMENT

Version: 1.0
Language: English

PURPOSE

Define the management framework for enterprise knowledge, semantic repositories and knowledge intelligence across the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES

- Centralize knowledge administration.
- Manage enterprise knowledge repositories.
- Control the complete knowledge lifecycle.
- Ensure operational reliability.
- Support governance policies.
- Preserve semantic consistency.
- Maximize knowledge accessibility.

MANAGEMENT DOMAINS

- Knowledge Repository Management.
- Knowledge Asset Management.
- Ontology Management.
- Knowledge Graph Management.
- Semantic Relationship Management.
- Metadata Management.
- Synchronization Management.
- Retention Management.
- Knowledge Audit.

MANAGEMENT PRINCIPLES

- Reliability.
- Scalability.
- Consistency.
- Security.
- Accountability.
- Transparency.
- Continuous Monitoring.
- Knowledge Integrity.

OPERATIONAL PROCESS

1. Register knowledge repositories.
2. Validate knowledge assets.
3. Classify knowledge.
4. Build semantic relationships.
5. Maintain ontology.
6. Synchronize distributed knowledge.
7. Monitor repository health.
8. Optimize indexing.
9. Archive obsolete knowledge.
10. Continuously improve knowledge quality.

QUALITY CONTROLS

- Knowledge validation.
- Semantic consistency verification.
- Ontology validation.
- Metadata quality assessment.
- Duplicate detection.
- Synchronization verification.
- Audit logging.
- Performance monitoring.

MANAGEMENT METRICS

- Repository availability.
- Knowledge quality score.
- Ontology consistency.
- Synchronization success rate.
- Duplicate reduction.
- Semantic accuracy.
- Retrieval performance.
- Operational efficiency.

FINAL DIRECTIVE

Knowledge Engine Management shall ensure secure, centralized, scalable and auditable management of all enterprise knowledge assets while preserving semantic intelligence and enabling continuous knowledge evolution throughout the GalSen IA ecosystem.
