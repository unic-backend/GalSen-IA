GALSEN IA
VOLET 21 - KNOWLEDGE ENGINE MANUAL
CHAPTER 02 - KNOWLEDGE ENGINE ARCHITECTURE

Version: 1.0
Language: English

PURPOSE

Define the architecture of the Knowledge Engine and its integration across the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES

- Provide a modular knowledge architecture.
- Centralize enterprise knowledge.
- Enable semantic reasoning.
- Ensure scalability and resilience.
- Support secure integrations.
- Simplify maintenance and future evolution.

ARCHITECTURE COMPONENTS

- Knowledge Core.
- Knowledge Repository.
- Knowledge Graph.
- Ontology Manager.
- Semantic Search Engine.
- Knowledge Validation Engine.
- Knowledge Inference Engine.
- Knowledge Index.
- Synchronization Service.
- Analytics Service.
- Monitoring Service.

ARCHITECTURE PRINCIPLES

- Modular Architecture.
- Semantic Intelligence.
- High Availability.
- Fault Tolerance.
- Secure Knowledge Storage.
- Explainability.
- Extensibility.

KNOWLEDGE FLOW

1. Receive information.
2. Validate knowledge.
3. Classify knowledge.
4. Build semantic relationships.
5. Store knowledge.
6. Index knowledge.
7. Synchronize repositories.
8. Execute semantic reasoning.
9. Deliver contextual knowledge.
10. Continuously improve the knowledge base.

QUALITY REQUIREMENTS

- High availability.
- Low retrieval latency.
- Semantic consistency.
- Data integrity.
- Scalability.
- Auditability.
- Performance monitoring.

FINAL DIRECTIVE

The Knowledge Engine architecture shall provide a secure, scalable, intelligent and semantically connected foundation for enterprise knowledge management throughout the GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 02
