GALSEN IA
VOLET 21 - KNOWLEDGE ENGINE MANUAL
CHAPTER 07 - KNOWLEDGE ENGINE COMPLIANCE

Version: 1.0
Language: English

PURPOSE

Define the compliance framework for enterprise knowledge, semantic repositories and knowledge intelligence across the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES

- Ensure regulatory compliance.
- Protect enterprise knowledge assets.
- Maintain audit readiness.
- Enforce governance policies.
- Preserve data privacy.
- Guarantee knowledge integrity.
- Support continuous compliance improvement.

COMPLIANCE PRINCIPLES

- Accountability.
- Transparency.
- Privacy by Design.
- Auditability.
- Knowledge Integrity.
- Responsible AI.
- Continuous Improvement.

COMPLIANCE DOMAINS

- Knowledge Governance.
- Data Protection.
- Knowledge Retention.
- Access Control.
- Metadata Compliance.
- Audit Logging.
- Regulatory Reporting.
- Privacy Management.
- Intellectual Property Protection.
- Risk Management.

COMPLIANCE PROCESS

1. Define compliance requirements.
2. Classify knowledge assets.
3. Apply governance policies.
4. Monitor compliance status.
5. Perform periodic audits.
6. Correct non-compliance.
7. Validate compliance evidence.
8. Improve compliance continuously.

QUALITY CONTROLS

- Policy validation.
- Compliance reporting.
- Audit verification.
- Risk assessments.
- Privacy impact assessments.
- Metadata verification.
- Governance reviews.
- Control effectiveness evaluation.

COMPLIANCE METRICS

- Compliance score.
- Audit success rate.
- Policy violation count.
- Knowledge integrity score.
- Data retention compliance.
- Privacy incident rate.
- Intellectual property compliance.
- Governance maturity.

FINAL DIRECTIVE

Knowledge Engine Compliance shall ensure that all enterprise knowledge, semantic repositories and knowledge intelligence operate in accordance with governance standards, security policies, privacy principles and regulatory obligations throughout the GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 07
