GALSEN IA VOLET 13 - NOTIFICATION ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 05 -
NOTIFICATION CHANNELS Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the standards for notification delivery channels across
the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES - Support multiple notification channels. - Ensure reliable
message delivery. - Standardize channel integration. - Protect
notification security. - Enable scalable notification services.

SUPPORTED CHANNELS - Email - SMS - Push Notifications - In-App
Notifications - Voice Services - Webhooks - Collaboration Platforms

CHANNEL PRINCIPLES - Reliability. - Security by design. -
Interoperability. - Scalability. - High availability.

CHANNEL MANAGEMENT PROCESS 1. Register channel. 2. Validate
configuration. 3. Authenticate connection. 4. Route notifications. 5.
Monitor channel health. 6. Optimize performance.

QUALITY CONTROLS - Delivery verification. - Availability monitoring. -
Error detection. - Audit logging. - Performance measurement.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Notification channels shall provide secure, reliable and
scalable delivery across all supported platforms within the GalSen IA
ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 05
