GALSEN IA VOLET 13 - NOTIFICATION ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 01 -
NOTIFICATION ENGINE VISION Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the vision, mission and strategic objectives of the
Notification Engine within the GalSen IA ecosystem.

VISION Provide intelligent, secure, timely and context-aware
notifications for users, agents and systems.

MISSION Deliver reliable notification services that improve awareness,
coordination and operational efficiency.

OBJECTIVES - Deliver notifications in real time. - Support multiple
delivery channels. - Prioritize critical events. - Personalize user
preferences. - Ensure scalability and reliability.

CORE PRINCIPLES - Timeliness. - Reliability. - Security. - Relevance. -
User-centric design. - Continuous improvement.

STRATEGIC CAPABILITIES - Event-driven notifications. - Multi-channel
delivery. - Intelligent prioritization. - Notification history. -
Preference management. - Analytics integration.

FINAL DIRECTIVE The Notification Engine shall provide secure, reliable
and intelligent notification capabilities across the entire GalSen IA
ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 01
