GALSEN IA VOLET 13 - NOTIFICATION ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 06 -
NOTIFICATION MONITORING Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the monitoring framework for notification services to
ensure availability, reliability and performance across the GalSen IA
ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES - Monitor notification health. - Detect delivery failures. -
Measure service performance. - Support proactive maintenance. - Improve
notification reliability.

MONITORING PRINCIPLES - Continuous observation. - Real-time
visibility. - Automated alerting. - Performance measurement. - Full
traceability.

MONITORING PROCESS 1. Collect notification metrics. 2. Detect anomalies.
3. Generate alerts. 4. Investigate incidents. 5. Apply corrective
actions. 6. Verify recovery.

KEY METRICS - Delivery success rate. - Delivery latency. - Channel
availability. - Error rate. - Queue utilization. - User engagement.

QUALITY CONTROLS - Health checks. - Alert validation. - Log analysis. -
Trend reporting. - Audit logging.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Notification monitoring shall provide continuous
visibility into notification services, ensuring secure, reliable and
scalable delivery throughout the GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 06
