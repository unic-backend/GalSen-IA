GALSEN IA VOLET 13 - NOTIFICATION ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 08 -
NOTIFICATION GOVERNANCE Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the governance framework for the Notification Engine
throughout its lifecycle.

OBJECTIVES - Ensure strategic alignment. - Maintain notification
quality. - Protect security and compliance. - Establish
accountability. - Drive continuous improvement.

GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLES - Transparency. - Accountability. - Policy-driven
management. - Standardization. - Continuous review.

ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES - Notification Governance Board. -
Notification Engine Owner. - Operations Team. - Security Team. -
Compliance Team. - System Architects.

GOVERNANCE PROCESS 1. Define notification policies. 2. Review changes.
3. Approve deployments. 4. Monitor governance metrics. 5. Audit
compliance. 6. Continuously improve governance.

POLICIES - Maintain version control. - Enforce access controls. -
Preserve audit trails. - Review notification services regularly. -
Retire obsolete notification assets safely.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Notification governance shall ensure that every
notification capability remains reliable, secure, traceable and aligned
with the long-term objectives of the GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 08
