GALSEN IA VOLET 13 - NOTIFICATION ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 03 -
NOTIFICATION LIFECYCLE Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the complete lifecycle of notifications from event
detection to archival within the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES - Standardize notification processing. - Ensure reliable
delivery. - Maintain notification integrity. - Support traceability. -
Enable continuous improvement.

LIFECYCLE STAGES 1. Event Detection 2. Notification Creation 3.
Validation 4. Priority Assignment 5. Channel Selection 6. Delivery 7.
Acknowledgement 8. Archival 9. Retention and Secure Deletion

LIFECYCLE PRINCIPLES - Reliability. - Timeliness. - Security. -
Traceability. - Scalability.

MANAGEMENT PRACTICES - Validate every notification. - Respect user
preferences. - Record delivery status. - Archive notification history. -
Remove expired records securely.

QUALITY CONTROLS - Delivery verification. - Duplicate prevention. -
Integrity validation. - Audit logging. - Performance monitoring.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Every notification shall follow a governed lifecycle
that guarantees secure, reliable and traceable delivery throughout the
GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 03
