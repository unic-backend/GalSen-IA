GALSEN IA VOLET 13 - NOTIFICATION ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 04 -
NOTIFICATION MANAGEMENT Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the standards and operational processes for managing
notifications across the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES - Standardize notification management. - Ensure reliable
notification delivery. - Maintain notification quality. - Reduce
operational risks. - Support continuous improvement.

MANAGEMENT PRINCIPLES - Centralized governance. - Secure notification
processing. - Consistent delivery. - Continuous monitoring. - Lifecycle
accountability.

MANAGEMENT PROCESS 1. Register notification sources. 2. Validate
notification policies. 3. Configure delivery rules. 4. Deliver
notifications. 5. Monitor notification services. 6. Optimize
performance. 7. Retire obsolete notification services.

OPERATIONAL CONTROLS - Configuration management. - Change management. -
Version control. - Incident tracking. - Performance monitoring.

QUALITY REQUIREMENTS - High availability. - Low delivery latency. -
Reliable notification routing. - Secure information exchange. - Complete
auditability.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Notification management shall ensure that every
notification service remains secure, reliable, maintainable and aligned
with the strategic objectives of the GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 04
