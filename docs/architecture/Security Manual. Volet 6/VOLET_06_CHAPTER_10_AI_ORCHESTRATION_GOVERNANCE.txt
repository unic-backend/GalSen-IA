GALSEN IA VOLET 6 - AI ORCHESTRATION MANUAL CHAPTER 10 - AI
ORCHESTRATION GOVERNANCE Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the governance framework that ensures the AI Orchestrator
remains reliable, secure, scalable and aligned with the long-term
mission of GalSen IA.

OBJECTIVES - Establish clear governance responsibilities. - Ensure
accountability. - Maintain operational excellence. - Manage risks. -
Support continuous evolution.

GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLES - Transparency. - Accountability. - Security by
design. - Continuous improvement. - Standards compliance.

ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES - AI Governance Board. - System Architects. -
AI Orchestrator Owner. - Security Team. - Operations Team. - Quality
Assurance Team.

GOVERNANCE PROCESS 1. Define orchestration policies. 2. Review
architectural changes. 3. Assess operational risks. 4. Approve major
updates. 5. Monitor governance metrics. 6. Audit compliance. 7.
Continuously improve governance practices.

AUDIT AND COMPLIANCE - Maintain complete audit logs. - Review policy
compliance regularly. - Track governance KPIs. - Document governance
decisions.

LONG-TERM EVOLUTION - Adapt governance to emerging technologies. -
Improve operational standards. - Preserve interoperability. - Maintain
backward compatibility when practical.

FINAL DIRECTIVE AI Orchestration Governance shall ensure that
orchestration remains trustworthy, resilient, efficient and
strategically aligned throughout the lifetime of GalSen IA.

END OF CHAPTER 10
