GALSEN IA VOLET 6 - AI ORCHESTRATION MANUAL CHAPTER 01 - AI
ORCHESTRATION VISION Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the vision, mission and strategic role of the AI
Orchestrator within the GalSen IA ecosystem.

VISION Create a unified intelligence layer that coordinates all AI
capabilities, services and knowledge into a consistent, reliable and
scalable system.

MISSION - Coordinate specialized AI agents. - Route tasks to the most
appropriate capabilities. - Ensure safe and trustworthy execution. -
Optimize performance and resource usage. - Deliver consistent user
experiences.

CORE PRINCIPLES - Modular architecture. - Human-centered design. -
Security by default. - Transparency. - Continuous learning. -
Scalability.

RESPONSIBILITIES - Understand user intent. - Plan execution
strategies. - Delegate tasks. - Aggregate results. - Resolve
conflicts. - Monitor execution quality.

SUCCESS CRITERIA - High response quality. - Low latency. - Reliable
orchestration. - Efficient resource utilization. - Continuous
improvement.

FINAL DIRECTIVE The AI Orchestrator serves as the central coordination
layer of GalSen IA, ensuring that every intelligent component operates
together efficiently, safely and consistently.

END OF CHAPTER 01
