GALSEN IA VOLET 12 - COMMUNICATION ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 10 -
COMMUNICATION ENGINE GOVERNANCE Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the overarching governance model for the Communication
Engine to ensure long-term sustainability, compliance and strategic
alignment.

OBJECTIVES - Ensure enterprise-wide governance. - Maintain operational
excellence. - Support regulatory compliance. - Enable continuous
evolution. - Align communication capabilities with business strategy.

GOVERNANCE FRAMEWORK - Strategic oversight. - Operational governance. -
Risk management. - Compliance management. - Performance management. -
Continuous improvement.

GOVERNANCE RESPONSIBILITIES - Define communication strategy. - Approve
major changes. - Monitor KPIs. - Review security and compliance. -
Manage lifecycle evolution. - Coordinate cross-engine integration.

SUCCESS METRICS - Communication availability. - Delivery reliability. -
Security compliance. - Operational efficiency. - User satisfaction. -
Continuous improvement outcomes.

FINAL DIRECTIVE The Communication Engine shall operate under a unified
governance framework that guarantees secure, reliable, scalable and
continuously improving communication services throughout the GalSen IA
ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 10
