GALSEN IA VOLET 12 - COMMUNICATION ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 04 -
COMMUNICATION MANAGEMENT Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the standards and operational processes for managing
communications across the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES - Standardize communication management. - Ensure reliable
message delivery. - Maintain communication quality. - Reduce operational
risks. - Support continuous improvement.

MANAGEMENT PRINCIPLES - Centralized governance. - Secure
communication. - Consistent delivery. - Continuous monitoring. -
Lifecycle accountability.

MANAGEMENT PROCESS 1. Register communication channels. 2. Validate
message policies. 3. Configure delivery rules. 4. Deliver
communications. 5. Monitor communication health. 6. Maintain and
optimize services. 7. Retire obsolete channels.

OPERATIONAL CONTROLS - Configuration management. - Change management. -
Version control. - Incident tracking. - Performance monitoring.

QUALITY REQUIREMENTS - High availability. - Low delivery latency. -
Reliable message routing. - Secure information exchange. - Complete
auditability.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Communication management shall ensure that every
communication service remains secure, reliable, maintainable and aligned
with the strategic objectives of the GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 04
