GALSEN IA VOLET 12 - COMMUNICATION ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 03 -
COMMUNICATION LIFECYCLE Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the complete lifecycle of communications within the
GalSen IA ecosystem from creation to archival.

OBJECTIVES - Standardize communication management. - Ensure reliable
message delivery. - Protect communication integrity. - Support secure
retention. - Enable continuous improvement.

LIFECYCLE STAGES 1. Message Creation 2. Validation 3. Authentication 4.
Routing 5. Delivery 6. Confirmation 7. Archival 8. Retention 9. Secure
Deletion

LIFECYCLE PRINCIPLES - Reliability. - Security by design. -
Traceability. - Scalability. - Continuous optimization.

MANAGEMENT PRACTICES - Validate every message. - Apply access
controls. - Maintain communication logs. - Archive important
exchanges. - Remove expired data securely.

QUALITY CONTROLS - Delivery verification. - Duplicate detection. -
Integrity validation. - Audit logging. - Performance monitoring.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Every communication shall follow a governed lifecycle
that guarantees secure, reliable and traceable exchanges across the
GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 03
