GALSEN IA VOLET 12 - COMMUNICATION ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 05 -
COMMUNICATION CHANNELS Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the standards for managing communication channels across
the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES - Support multiple communication channels. - Ensure reliable
message delivery. - Standardize channel integration. - Protect
communication security. - Enable scalable communications.

SUPPORTED CHANNELS - Email - SMS - Push Notifications - In-App
Messaging - Voice Services - Webhooks - External Messaging Platforms

CHANNEL PRINCIPLES - Reliability. - Security by design. -
Interoperability. - Scalability. - High availability.

CHANNEL MANAGEMENT PROCESS 1. Register channel. 2. Validate
configuration. 3. Authenticate connection. 4. Route messages. 5. Monitor
channel health. 6. Optimize performance.

QUALITY CONTROLS - Delivery verification. - Channel availability
monitoring. - Error detection. - Audit logging. - Performance
measurement.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Communication channels shall provide secure, reliable
and scalable message delivery across all supported platforms within the
GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 05
