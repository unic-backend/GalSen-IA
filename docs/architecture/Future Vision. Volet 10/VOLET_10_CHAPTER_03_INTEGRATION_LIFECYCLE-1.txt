GALSEN IA VOLET 10 - INTEGRATION ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 03 - INTEGRATION
LIFECYCLE Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the complete lifecycle of integrations within the GalSen
IA ecosystem from design to retirement.

OBJECTIVES - Standardize integration management. - Ensure reliable
deployment. - Maintain interoperability. - Support secure evolution. -
Enable continuous improvement.

LIFECYCLE STAGES 1. Planning 2. Design 3. Development 4. Validation 5.
Deployment 6. Monitoring 7. Maintenance 8. Versioning 9. Retirement

LIFECYCLE PRINCIPLES - Reliability. - Security by design. -
Traceability. - Scalability. - Continuous optimization.

MANAGEMENT PRACTICES - Document every integration. - Apply version
control. - Monitor health continuously. - Review performance
regularly. - Retire obsolete integrations safely.

QUALITY CONTROLS - Interface validation. - Compatibility testing. -
Security verification. - Performance monitoring. - Audit logging.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Every integration shall follow a governed lifecycle that
guarantees secure, reliable and scalable interoperability across the
GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 03
