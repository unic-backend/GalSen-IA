GALSEN IA VOLET 10 - INTEGRATION ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 05 - INTEGRATION
SYNCHRONIZATION Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define how data synchronization is performed across internal
modules and external systems within the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES - Ensure consistent data. - Minimize synchronization
latency. - Prevent conflicts. - Maintain reliability. - Support scalable
integrations.

SYNCHRONIZATION PRINCIPLES - Consistency. - Reliability. -
Idempotency. - Security by design. - Fault tolerance.

SYNCHRONIZATION PROCESS 1. Detect changes. 2. Validate data. 3. Resolve
conflicts. 4. Synchronize records. 5. Verify completion. 6. Log
synchronization events.

SYNCHRONIZATION MODES - Real-time. - Scheduled. - Event-driven. - Batch
processing.

QUALITY CONTROLS - Data integrity verification. - Conflict detection. -
Retry mechanisms. - Performance monitoring. - Audit logging.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Integration synchronization shall ensure secure,
reliable and consistent data exchange across the entire GalSen IA
ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 05
