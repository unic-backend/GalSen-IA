GALSEN IA VOLET 10 - INTEGRATION ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 06 - INTEGRATION
MONITORING Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define how integrations are monitored to ensure availability,
performance and reliability across the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES - Detect integration issues early. - Monitor operational
health. - Measure performance continuously. - Improve service
reliability. - Support proactive maintenance.

MONITORING PRINCIPLES - Continuous observation. - Real-time
visibility. - Automated alerting. - Performance measurement. -
Traceability.

MONITORING PROCESS 1. Collect operational metrics. 2. Detect anomalies.
3. Generate alerts. 4. Investigate incidents. 5. Apply corrective
actions. 6. Verify recovery.

KEY METRICS - Availability. - Response time. - Synchronization success
rate. - Error rate. - Throughput. - Resource utilization.

QUALITY CONTROLS - Health checks. - Alert validation. - Log analysis. -
Trend reporting. - Audit logging.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Integration monitoring shall provide continuous
visibility into integration health, enabling reliable, secure and
scalable interoperability throughout the GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 06
