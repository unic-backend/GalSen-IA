GALSEN IA VOLET 10 - INTEGRATION ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 04 - INTEGRATION
MANAGEMENT Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the standards and operational processes for managing
integrations throughout the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES - Standardize integration management. - Ensure reliable
operations. - Maintain interoperability. - Reduce operational risks. -
Support continuous improvement.

MANAGEMENT PRINCIPLES - Centralized governance. - Standardized
interfaces. - Secure communication. - Continuous monitoring. - Lifecycle
accountability.

MANAGEMENT PROCESS 1. Register integration. 2. Validate interface
specifications. 3. Configure authentication. 4. Deploy integration. 5.
Monitor operational health. 6. Maintain and update. 7. Retire obsolete
integrations.

OPERATIONAL CONTROLS - Configuration management. - Change management. -
Version control. - Incident tracking. - Performance monitoring.

QUALITY REQUIREMENTS - High availability. - Low latency. - Reliable
synchronization. - Secure data exchange. - Complete auditability.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Integration management shall ensure that every
integration remains secure, reliable, maintainable and aligned with the
strategic objectives of the GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 04
