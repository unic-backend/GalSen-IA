GALSEN IA VOLET 10 - INTEGRATION ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 01 - INTEGRATION
ENGINE VISION Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the vision, mission and strategic role of the Integration
Engine within the GalSen IA ecosystem.

VISION Build a secure, scalable and interoperable integration platform
that connects internal modules, external services and AI systems
seamlessly.

MISSION - Connect all GalSen IA components. - Enable secure data
exchange. - Simplify interoperability. - Support automation across
systems. - Ensure reliable integrations.

CORE PRINCIPLES - Interoperability. - Security by design. -
Scalability. - Reliability. - Modularity. - Standardization.

RESPONSIBILITIES - Manage internal integrations. - Connect external
APIs. - Synchronize data. - Monitor integration health. - Support
automated workflows.

SUCCESS CRITERIA - High integration reliability. - Low synchronization
latency. - Secure communications. - Stable interoperability. -
Continuous optimization.

FINAL DIRECTIVE The Integration Engine shall serve as the connectivity
layer that enables secure, reliable and scalable communication across
the entire GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 01
