GALSEN IA VOLET 11 - SECURITY ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 07 - SECURITY
COMPLIANCE Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the compliance framework that ensures security practices
align with legal, regulatory and organizational requirements across the
GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES - Ensure regulatory compliance. - Enforce security
policies. - Protect sensitive information. - Maintain audit readiness. -
Support continuous compliance improvement.

COMPLIANCE PRINCIPLES - Accountability. - Transparency. - Risk-based
governance. - Continuous verification. - Evidence-based compliance.

COMPLIANCE PROCESS 1. Identify applicable requirements. 2. Assess
compliance status. 3. Implement required controls. 4. Monitor compliance
continuously. 5. Perform audits. 6. Remediate identified gaps.

COMPLIANCE CONTROLS - Policy enforcement. - Access reviews. - Data
protection. - Audit logging. - Documentation management.

QUALITY CONTROLS - Compliance assessments. - Internal audits. - External
audit readiness. - Corrective action tracking. - Continuous policy
review.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Security compliance shall ensure that all security
operations remain aligned with applicable regulations, internal policies
and the long-term governance objectives of the GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 07
