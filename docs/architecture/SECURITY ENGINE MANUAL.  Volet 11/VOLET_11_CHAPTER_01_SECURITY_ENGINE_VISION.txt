GALSEN IA VOLET 11 - SECURITY ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 01 - SECURITY ENGINE
VISION Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the vision, mission and strategic role of the Security
Engine within the GalSen IA ecosystem.

VISION Build a secure, resilient and trusted security platform that
protects every component, user, service and data asset across GalSen IA.

MISSION - Protect digital assets. - Detect and respond to threats. -
Enforce security policies. - Support regulatory compliance. - Enable
secure innovation.

CORE PRINCIPLES - Security by design. - Zero Trust. - Least privilege. -
Defense in depth. - Continuous monitoring. - Resilience.

RESPONSIBILITIES - Identity and access protection. - Threat detection. -
Incident response. - Security monitoring. - Policy enforcement. - Risk
reduction.

SUCCESS CRITERIA - Strong protection. - Rapid threat detection. - High
availability. - Regulatory compliance. - Continuous improvement.

FINAL DIRECTIVE The Security Engine shall serve as the protection layer
that safeguards every system, workflow and data exchange across the
entire GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 01
