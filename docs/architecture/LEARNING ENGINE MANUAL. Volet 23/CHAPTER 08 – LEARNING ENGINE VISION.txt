CHAPTER 08 - LEARNING ENGINE GOVERNANCE

Version: 1.0
Language: English

PURPOSE

Define the governance framework for enterprise learning services, adaptive intelligence and continuous AI improvement across the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES

- Establish governance responsibilities.
- Ensure strategic alignment.
- Maintain security and compliance.
- Support accountability.
- Govern enterprise learning.
- Promote responsible AI.
- Drive continuous improvement.

GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLES

- Accountability.
- Transparency.
- Explainability.
- Policy-Driven Management.
- Responsible AI.
- Risk Awareness.
- Learning Integrity.
- Continuous Review.

ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

- Enterprise Learning Governance Board.
- Chief Learning Officer.
- AI Learning Architects.
- Model Management Team.
- Data Science Team.
- Security Team.
- Compliance Officers.
- AI Ethics Committee.
- Audit Team.

GOVERNANCE DOMAINS

- Learning Governance.
- AI Governance.
- Model Governance.
- Training Governance.
- Data Governance.
- Risk Governance.
- Compliance Governance.
- Performance Governance.
- Learning Lifecycle Governance.
- Quality Governance.

GOVERNANCE PROCESS

1. Define governance policies.
2. Approve enterprise learning standards.
3. Validate learning models.
4. Review training strategies.
5. Monitor governance metrics.
6. Audit compliance.
7. Assess operational risks.
8. Validate learning quality.
9. Improve governance continuously.

QUALITY CONTROLS

- Governance reviews.
- Policy validation.
- Learning quality audits.
- Model verification.
- Training verification.
- Risk assessments.
- Compliance monitoring.
- Continuous governance assessment.

SUCCESS METRICS

- Governance maturity.
- Learning quality score.
- Model performance index.
- AI explainability score.
- Policy compliance rate.
- Audit success rate.
- Operational efficiency.
- Continuous improvement index.

FINAL DIRECTIVE

Learning Engine Governance shall ensure secure, accountable, transparent and policy-driven governance of all enterprise learning services while maintaining trusted AI learning, business alignment and continuous organizational intelligence throughout the GalSen IA ecosystem.
