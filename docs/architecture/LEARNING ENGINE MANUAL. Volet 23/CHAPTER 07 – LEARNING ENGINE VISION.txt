GALSEN IA
VOLET 23 - LEARNING ENGINE MANUAL
CHAPTER 07 - LEARNING ENGINE COMPLIANCE

Version: 1.0
Language: English

PURPOSE

Define the compliance framework for enterprise learning services, adaptive intelligence and AI learning processes across the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES

- Ensure regulatory compliance.
- Protect enterprise learning assets.
- Maintain audit readiness.
- Enforce governance policies.
- Preserve transparency and accountability.
- Guarantee learning integrity.
- Support continuous compliance improvement.

COMPLIANCE PRINCIPLES

- Accountability.
- Transparency.
- Explainability.
- Privacy by Design.
- Auditability.
- Responsible AI.
- Learning Integrity.
- Continuous Improvement.

COMPLIANCE DOMAINS

- Learning Governance.
- AI Governance.
- Training Compliance.
- Model Compliance.
- Data Protection.
- Access Control.
- Audit Logging.
- Regulatory Reporting.
- Privacy Management.
- Risk Management.

COMPLIANCE PROCESS

1. Define compliance requirements.
2. Classify learning assets.
3. Apply governance policies.
4. Validate learning models.
5. Monitor compliance status.
6. Perform periodic audits.
7. Correct non-compliance.
8. Validate compliance evidence.
9. Improve compliance continuously.

QUALITY CONTROLS

- Policy validation.
- Compliance reporting.
- Audit verification.
- Risk assessments.
- Privacy impact assessments.
- AI ethics reviews.
- Governance verification.
- Control effectiveness evaluation.

COMPLIANCE METRICS

- Compliance score.
- Audit success rate.
- Policy violation count.
- Learning integrity score.
- AI explainability score.
- Privacy incident rate.
- Regulatory conformity.
- Governance maturity.

FINAL DIRECTIVE

Learning Engine Compliance shall ensure that all enterprise learning services, adaptive intelligence and AI learning processes operate in accordance with governance standards, security policies, responsible AI principles and regulatory obligations throughout the GalSen IA ecosystem.

END OF CHAPTER 07
