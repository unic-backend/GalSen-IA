VOLET 23 - LEARNING ENGINE MANUAL
CHAPTER 10 - LEARNING ENGINE GOVERNANCE

Version: 1.0
Language: English

PURPOSE

Define the enterprise governance model for the Learning Engine to ensure long-term sustainability, operational excellence, trusted AI learning and strategic alignment throughout the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES

- Ensure enterprise-wide learning governance.
- Maintain operational excellence.
- Support regulatory compliance.
- Enable continuous learning evolution.
- Align learning services with business strategy.
- Protect enterprise learning assets.
- Promote trusted, transparent and explainable AI learning.

GOVERNANCE FRAMEWORK

- Strategic Oversight.
- Operational Governance.
- Learning Lifecycle Governance.
- AI Governance.
- Model Governance.
- Data Governance.
- Risk Governance.
- Compliance Management.
- Performance Management.
- Continuous Improvement.

GOVERNANCE RESPONSIBILITIES

- Define enterprise learning strategy.
- Approve major learning architecture changes.
- Govern AI model evolution.
- Monitor learning KPIs.
- Review security and compliance.
- Coordinate enterprise learning initiatives.
- Manage training standards.
- Drive continuous optimization.

SUCCESS METRICS

- Learning availability.
- Model accuracy.
- Learning efficiency.
- Adaptation speed.
- Explainability score.
- Security compliance.
- Operational efficiency.
- Knowledge transfer rate.
- Business value creation.
- Continuous improvement index.

CONTINUOUS GOVERNANCE

- Continuous policy review.
- Learning quality assessments.
- AI model reviews.
- Governance maturity assessments.
- Enterprise learning audits.
- Strategic roadmap reviews.
- Risk governance reviews.
- AI ethics assessments.

FINAL DIRECTIVE

The Learning Engine shall operate under a unified governance framework that guarantees secure, reliable, scalable, explainable and continuously improving enterprise learning services while preserving trusted AI learning, organizational intelligence and strategic business alignment throughout the GalSen IA ecosystem.
