VOLET 23 - LEARNING ENGINE MANUAL
CHAPTER 04 - LEARNING ENGINE MANAGEMENT

Version: 1.0
Language: English

PURPOSE

Define the management framework for enterprise learning services, adaptive intelligence and continuous optimization across the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES

- Centralize learning administration.
- Manage learning repositories.
- Control the learning lifecycle.
- Ensure operational reliability.
- Support governance policies.
- Preserve learning quality.
- Maximize adaptive intelligence.

MANAGEMENT DOMAINS

- Learning Repository Management.
- Experience Management.
- Feedback Management.
- Pattern Management.
- Model Management.
- Training Management.
- Learning Analytics.
- Learning Audit.
- Learning Lifecycle Management.

MANAGEMENT PRINCIPLES

- Reliability.
- Scalability.
- Explainability.
- Accountability.
- Transparency.
- Security.
- Continuous Monitoring.
- Continuous Improvement.

OPERATIONAL PROCESS

1. Register learning events.
2. Validate learning data.
3. Classify experiences.
4. Train learning models.
5. Validate learning outputs.
6. Monitor learning performance.
7. Optimize learning models.
8. Record learning history.
9. Archive completed learning assets.

QUALITY CONTROLS

- Learning validation.
- Model verification.
- Experience quality assessment.
- Audit logging.
- Performance monitoring.
- Continuous optimization.
- Explainability verification.

FINAL DIRECTIVE

Learning Engine Management shall ensure secure, centralized, scalable and auditable management of all enterprise learning services while maintaining adaptive intelligence and continuous optimization throughout the GalSen IA ecosystem.
