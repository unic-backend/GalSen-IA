GALSEN IA VOLET 8 - WORKFLOW ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 10 - WORKFLOW ENGINE
GOVERNANCE Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the governance framework that oversees the Workflow
Engine throughout its lifecycle.

OBJECTIVES - Ensure strategic alignment. - Maintain workflow quality. -
Protect security and compliance. - Ensure accountability. - Support
continuous evolution.

GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLES - Transparency. - Accountability. - Security by
design. - Policy-driven management. - Continuous improvement.

ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES - Workflow Governance Board. - Workflow
Engine Owner. - Operations Team. - Security Team. - Compliance Team. -
System Architects.

GOVERNANCE PROCESS 1. Define governance policies. 2. Review major
workflow changes. 3. Assess operational risks. 4. Approve deployments.
5. Monitor governance metrics. 6. Audit compliance. 7. Continuously
improve governance.

AUDIT AND COMPLIANCE - Maintain audit trails. - Track governance KPIs. -
Review policy compliance. - Document governance decisions.

LONG-TERM EVOLUTION - Adapt governance to emerging technologies. -
Improve workflow standards. - Preserve interoperability. - Maintain
backward compatibility where practical.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Workflow Engine Governance shall ensure that every
workflow remains reliable, secure, traceable and aligned with the
long-term mission of GalSen IA.

END OF CHAPTER 10
