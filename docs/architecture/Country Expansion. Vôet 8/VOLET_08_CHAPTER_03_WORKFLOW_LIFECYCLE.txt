GALSEN IA VOLET 8 - WORKFLOW ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 03 - WORKFLOW
LIFECYCLE Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the complete lifecycle of workflows from creation to
retirement within the GalSen IA ecosystem.

OBJECTIVES - Standardize workflow management. - Ensure reliable
execution. - Support continuous optimization. - Maintain traceability. -
Enable scalable automation.

LIFECYCLE STAGES 1. Design 2. Validation 3. Deployment 4. Execution 5.
Monitoring 6. Optimization 7. Versioning 8. Retirement

LIFECYCLE PRINCIPLES - Controlled evolution. - Full traceability. -
Version management. - Security by design. - Continuous improvement.

WORKFLOW MANAGEMENT - Define workflow metadata. - Track execution
history. - Manage workflow versions. - Archive obsolete workflows. -
Review lifecycle regularly.

QUALITY CONTROLS - Execution success rate. - Workflow consistency. -
Performance metrics. - Error frequency. - Compliance verification.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Every workflow shall follow a governed lifecycle that
ensures reliability, efficiency, security and long-term maintainability
across GalSen IA.
