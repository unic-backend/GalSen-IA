GALSEN IA VOLET 8 - WORKFLOW ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 09 - WORKFLOW QUALITY
AND OPTIMIZATION Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the standards and continuous improvement processes that
ensure workflow quality, efficiency and scalability.

OBJECTIVES - Improve workflow reliability. - Optimize execution
performance. - Reduce operational complexity. - Ensure consistent
automation. - Support long-term evolution.

QUALITY PRINCIPLES - Accuracy before speed. - Consistent execution. -
Continuous validation. - Measurable performance. - Ongoing optimization.

QUALITY METRICS - Workflow success rate - Execution latency - Failure
rate - Resource utilization - Automation coverage - User satisfaction

OPTIMIZATION PROCESS 1. Collect workflow metrics. 2. Identify
bottlenecks. 3. Prioritize improvements. 4. Implement optimizations. 5.
Validate results. 6. Monitor long-term impact.

CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT - Perform regular workflow audits. - Eliminate
redundant steps. - Optimize execution paths. - Improve automation
efficiency. - Adapt workflows to changing business needs.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Workflow quality and optimization shall ensure that
every workflow remains reliable, efficient, scalable and aligned with
the strategic objectives of GalSen IA.

END OF CHAPTER 09
