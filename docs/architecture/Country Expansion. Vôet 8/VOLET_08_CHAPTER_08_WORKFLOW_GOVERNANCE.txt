GALSEN IA VOLET 8 - WORKFLOW ENGINE MANUAL CHAPTER 08 - WORKFLOW
GOVERNANCE Version: 1.0 Language: English

PURPOSE Define the governance framework that manages the Workflow Engine
throughout its lifecycle.

OBJECTIVES - Ensure accountable workflow management. - Maintain workflow
quality. - Protect security and compliance. - Standardize operational
practices. - Support continuous improvement.

GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLES - Clear ownership. - Transparency. -
Accountability. - Policy-driven management. - Continuous review.

ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES - Workflow Owner - Workflow Administrator -
Operations Team - Security Team - Compliance Team - System Architects

GOVERNANCE PROCESS 1. Define workflow policies. 2. Review major workflow
changes. 3. Approve deployments. 4. Monitor governance metrics. 5. Audit
compliance. 6. Continuously improve governance.

POLICIES - Apply version control. - Enforce approval processes. -
Preserve audit history. - Review inactive workflows. - Retire obsolete
workflows safely.

FINAL DIRECTIVE Workflow governance shall ensure that every workflow
remains reliable, secure, traceable and aligned with the long-term
objectives of GalSen IA.

END OF CHAPTER 08
